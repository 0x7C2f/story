# Style Sheet

The book's house decisions, kept the way a copyeditor keeps them. Read this before drafting or revising prose. `story prose` enforces the lists in the frontmatter: `dialect` (british, american, or unspecified) flags the other dialect's common spellings, each `preferred` entry flags its `avoid` form, `watch-words` are counted in every chapter, and `allow-words` silences a built-in filter word or adverb. Add a `samples` list of your own prose (`../book-one`, approved chapters) and `story prose` compares each chapter with it instead of fixed limits.

This sheet is the authority on voice, spelling, dating, and terminology. [voice-sample.md](voice-sample.md) is the quick-read version.

## Voice

The narrative distance is close third-person limited, focusing intimately on Elias's sensory and psychological experiences. Sentences blend technical, procedural language with atmospheric, evocative descriptions of the futuristic setting. The tone is suspenseful and grounded, avoiding overly flowery language in favor of sharp, concrete details.

"He leaned closer to the monitor, his breath catching in his throat. Memories were supposed to be immutable, protected by layers of cryptographic seals."

## Spelling And Usage

- **catalog**: Preferred American spelling over 'catalogue'.
- **toward**: Preferred over 'towards'.

Titles, ranks, institutions, invented terms, and deities. Invented terms also belong in the glossary.

## Hyphenation And Compounds

- memory-archivist (when used as a compound modifier, though usually open as 'memory archivist')

## Numbers, Dates, And Time

Spelled-out or numerals, and how in-world dates and times are written.
- Numerals for specific years (e.g., 2042).

## Dialogue And Punctuation

Quote marks, dash style, ellipses, italics for thought or foreign words, and the default dialogue tags.
- Use em-dashes (—) without spaces for interruptions or parenthetical thoughts.
- Italics for system messages (e.g., *Access Denied*).

## Character Voices

**Elias Thorne**: Analytical, observant. His internal monologue often reflects his procedural training, mixed with an underlying paranoia.

**Kaelen**: Clipped, fast, cynical. Efficiency over politeness; addresses Elias by surname; states odds as facts and facts as orders.

**Jarek**: A low gravel rumble, short declaratives, the vocabulary of survival — scrap, water, dust, weight. Repeats a supplicant's own word back at them as a test.

**Director Vane**: Unheard so far. Her voice reaches the page only through machine messages (*Access Denied. Clearance Level 5 Required.*). When she speaks, keep the register administrative, unhurried, and certain.
