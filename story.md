# The Glass Circuit

## Synopsis

In the sprawling, neon-drenched metropolis of Neo-Veridia, memory is a commodity traded on the black market. Elias Thorne, a rogue mnemonic extractor, discovers a shattered fragment of a memory that doesn't belong to any human, pointing to a vast corporate conspiracy. As he delves deeper, he must navigate a deadly web of synthetic assassins and corporate espionage to uncover the truth about his own past.

## Tone & Style

Gritty, atmospheric, and fast-paced. The prose should be sharp, leaning into sensory details of a high-tech, low-life world: the hum of neon, the tang of ozone, the pervasive dampness of the lower levels. The emotional register is cynical but tinged with a desperate search for authenticity.

## Notes

Initial premise established. The story focuses on Elias Thorne's journey to uncover the truth behind the synthetic memory.
