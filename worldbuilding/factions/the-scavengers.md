# The Scavengers

## Description
A hardy community of exiles and survivors living in the Outskirts beyond the control of the Oversight Committee. They rely on salvaged technology and repurposed industrial waste to survive the harsh environment.

## Hierarchy & Structure
They operate as a loose collective with a focus on mutual defense and resource sharing. Leadership is often informal, based on experience and capability in navigating the Outskirts.

## Agenda
Survival and independence from the sterile, controlled environment of New Providence. They hold deep suspicion toward city dwellers and the Oversight Committee, viewing them as architects of oppression.
