# Oversight Committee

## Purpose

The Oversight Committee is the governing body responsible for maintaining the "integrity" of digitized memories within New Providence. Their stated purpose is to protect citizens from psychological trauma by curating history, but their true goal is to control the narrative of society by selectively altering or suppressing unfavorable events, maintaining their grip on power.

## Power Base

They wield absolute control over the Nexus Archives and the data it contains. They possess advanced cryptographic splicing technology capable of altering memories without fracturing a subject's psyche. They also command a private security force, the Archive sentinels, authorized to use lethal suppression against threats to their narrative control.

## Members

- **Archive Sentinels:** Heavily armed, mechanized enforcers deployed to maintain security and eliminate intruders or rogue archivists.

## Conflicts

They are currently facing a critical breach: Elias Thorne, a rogue memory archivist, has stolen a drive containing memory fragment M-774-B, a piece of unaltered history that exposes their manipulation. Their immediate external pressure is tracking down and neutralizing Thorne before the unedited memory can be distributed.
