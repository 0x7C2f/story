# Oversight Committee

## Overview

The powerful, shadowy regulatory body tasked with managing the Nexus Archives and overseeing the digital preservation of human memory in New Providence. Outwardly, they are a benevolent government organization ensuring societal stability.

## Purpose

To control the narrative of history by curating, and when deemed necessary, fundamentally rewriting memories to maintain social order and protect corporate interests.

## Structure and Leadership

The Committee is governed by a small circle of high-ranking directors, each overseeing different sectors of data management. The leadership operates with absolute secrecy, employing heavily armed corporate enforcers to execute their directives.

**The Directorate** consists of five key figures, with Director Vane being the most prominent known operative involved in the memory alteration black sites.

## Scale of Operations

Recent evidence (such as Elias's discovery of the batch protocol metadata) reveals that the Committee's memory modification is not limited to isolated, high-profile cover-ups. They operate an industrial-scale fabrication ring, utilizing a hidden network of black-site modification labs. In these labs, they leverage subjects' own neural pathways to weave fabricated histories into their minds, ensuring seamless, undetectable alterations.

## Methods and Resources

They possess unparalleled access to the Nexus Archives and utilize highly classified black-site modification labs (like the one seen in memory M-774-B). Their resources include advanced cryptography, neural-interface technology, and a ubiquitous surveillance network that actively monitors and quarantines unauthorized data access across the upper district's network.

## Relationship to the Story

The primary antagonist faction. They are responsible for the altered memory Elias discovers. Their industrial-scale fabrication of history serves as the overarching threat Elias and Kaelen must dismantle.
