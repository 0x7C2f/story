# Signal Repeaters

## Description

Six sleek, matte-black, high-density components, surprisingly heavy for their size. They are built to pre-collapse military specifications, fully shielded against ambient radiation and harsh environmental conditions. Having been locked inside a reinforced cabinet in the Dead Zone's relay station, they are pristine and untouched by decay.

## Function

They serve as high-capacity network relays capable of bouncing a localized signal over vast distances and through significant interference. Elias plans to use them to create a stable, decentralized mesh network capable of punching through the Oversight Committee's perimeter jamming to reach the Undercity.

## History

Manufactured before the collapse and the subsequent walling off of the city, they were intended for a regional communications grid that was abandoned before completion. They sat dormant for decades in the Dead Zone until Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek recovered them in chapter 18.

## Current State

Active. Extracted from the relay station by Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek in chapter 18, they are currently in the possession of the resistance at the Scrapyard Settlement, awaiting integration into a new shadow network.
