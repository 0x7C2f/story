# Loyalist Transport

## Appearance

A light armored personnel carrier used by the Oversight Committee's sweep teams. It features thick ferrocrete-reinforced plating, cold white headlights, and a repulsor-lift drive system.

## History & Significance

Originally deployed by Director Vane as part of Team Alpha to sweep the Outskirts perimeter and locate the source of the resistance's counter-broadcast. It was captured by Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek during an ambush at the old transit bridge in the eastern ravine.

## Mechanism or Usage

Operates on a standard repulsor array and main drive. Following the ambush, its rear repulsor lift was damaged by a shaped charge, but the main drive remains intact, requiring scrap and repairs to be fully functional for the resistance.
