# Primary Signal Repeater

## Description

A thick, metallic spire bristling with antenna arrays, mounted on the roof of a bunker-like structure constructed of heavy ferrocrete and composite plating in the center of the Sector Six staging ground.

## Function

It acts as the master node for Director Vane's mobile rewrite network. By synchronizing the signals of the secondary transports, it can carpet-bomb a localized area (like the Outskirts) with a concentrated memory wipe, resetting the population to a baseline compliance state.

## History

Deployed by Director Vane after the failure of the central broadcast tower synchronization. It represents a shift from a city-wide overwrite to targeted, localized erasure to crush the nascent resistance.

## Current State

Currently active and marshaling secondary transports at the Sector Six staging ground. It is the primary target of Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek's assault in Chapter 24.
