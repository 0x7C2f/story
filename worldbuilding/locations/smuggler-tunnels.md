# Smuggler Tunnels

## Description

A vast, unmapped network of subterranean tunnels running beneath the sectors, originally used for maintenance before being co-opted by smugglers and scavengers.

## History

These tunnels were officially sealed off by the Oversight Committee decades ago, but locals have maintained secret access points to bypass sector checkpoints.

## Current State

Currently serving as an escape route for Elias and Kaelen after the destruction of Sector Five.
