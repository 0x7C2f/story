# Old Transit Bridge

## Description

A massive, rusted structure of reinforced ferrocrete and durasteel that spans the deep eastern ravine dividing the city limits from the deeper Outskirts. Originally part of New Providence's expansion infrastructure, it was abandoned decades ago when the city contracted. The roadway is cracked, choked with dust and twisted rebar, while the rusted stanchions groan under the weight of the high winds.

## History

Before the Outskirts became a dumping ground for the city's unwanted, the transit bridge was intended to connect a planned commercial district. The project failed due to supply shortages and shifting political priorities. For years, Scavengers have used it sparingly, stripping it of any accessible salvage.

## Culture & Customs

To the Scavengers, the bridge is an exposed, dangerous chokepoint. To the city's sweep teams, it is the only reliable crossing for heavy transports moving into Sector Five of the Outskirts.

## Notable Features

- **The Main Span:** A narrow, exposed roadway littered with debris, forcing vehicles into a single-file crawl.
- **The Undercarriage:** A latticework of maintenance gantries and rusted support struts, providing cover and high ground for an ambush.
- **The Ravine:** A precipitous drop into jagged scrap and darkness, making the bridge the only viable path across.

## Current State

The bridge serves as the site of the resistance's first major ambush against the loyalist sweep teams deployed by Director Vane. By holding the high ground in the undercarriage, Elias and the Scavengers turn the bridge's narrow span into a fatal chokepoint.
