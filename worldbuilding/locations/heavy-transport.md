# Heavy Transport

## Description

A massive armored ground transport vehicle originally used by the Committee to move prisoners from Sector Six. The interior is cramped and smells of ozone and old sweat. The exterior is reinforced durasteel with Committee markings.

## History

Hijacked by Kaelen from the Sector Six staging ground to rescue Elias. Currently being used to infiltrate the Nexus Spire.

## Culture & Customs

None.

## Notable Features

Spoofed transponder beacon, heavy reinforced plating, shielded crew compartment.

## Current State

Active and carrying Elias and Kaelen towards the Nexus Spire.
