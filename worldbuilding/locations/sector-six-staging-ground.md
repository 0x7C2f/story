# Sector Six Staging Ground

## Description

An old distribution hub on the edge of the Dead Zone, composed of reinforced ferrocrete and rusted steel gantries. Harsh floodlights illuminate rows of identical heavy transports lined up in the perpetual twilight. The air smells of ozone and old dust.

## History

A former distribution center that fell into disrepair, now repurposed by the Oversight Committee as a staging ground.

## Culture & Customs

Strict military discipline, focused entirely on the rapid deployment of a mobile rewrite protocol.

## Notable Features

- **Heavy Transports:** Dozens of identical crawlers lined up, many being fitted with massive, cylindrical signal repeaters.
- **Cooling Towers:** Large, shadowed structures providing cover off the main thoroughfare.
- **Central Command Node:** A brutalist block of concrete at the heart of the hub, bristling with communication antennae, housing the central synchronization server.

## Current State

Director Vane is actively marshaling heavy transports here to anchor a mobile network for a localized rewrite protocol targeted at the Outskirts.
