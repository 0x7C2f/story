# Sector Six Staging Ground

## Description

An industrial fortress looming against the smog-choked sky on the outer perimeter of Sector Six. The perimeter is ringed with high-tension mesh and massive steel barricades that retract into reinforced concrete walls. The yard is a chaotic expanse of pulverized ferrocrete, reeking of promethium fuel and exhaust, and filled with precise rows of heavy transports idling with a low, collective thrum. In the center stands a bunker-like structure constructed of heavy ferrocrete and composite plating, topped with a thick, metallic spire bristling with antenna arrays.

## History

Once a logistical nerve center and distribution hub for the lower tiers, it has been repurposed and fortified by the Oversight Committee into a military staging ground. The old sub-level maintenance tunnels that used to move supplies underground still exist near the southern loading docks.

## Culture & Customs

A hive of controlled, military precision. Operations are highly automated, with localized scanning beams, transponder pings, and rolling command ciphers securing the perimeter, backed by fixed automated turrets.

## Notable Features

- **The Master Node Bunker**: A heavily guarded, bunker-like structure in the center of the yard, housing the power core for the primary signal repeater.
- **The Primary Signal Repeater**: The metallic spire on the bunker's roof.
- **Sub-level Access Point**: A heavily oxidized door near the southern loading docks, secured by a modern mag-lock, leading to downward-sloping concrete maintenance corridors.
- **Heavy Steel Barricades**: The main entrance, controlled by a security mainframe that checks transponders against a rolling command cipher.

## Current State

Currently marshaling dozens of heavy transports to form a mobile rewrite network. Loyalist troops patrol the yard, preparing to deploy a concentrated memory wipe against the Outskirts. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek have infiltrated the grounds via a captured transport and are attempting to reach the master node.
