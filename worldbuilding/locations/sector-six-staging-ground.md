# Sector Six Staging Ground

## Description

An old distribution hub situated on the edge of the Dead Zone, repurposed into a fortified staging ground by the loyalist factions of the Oversight Committee. The area is dominated by heavy ferrocrete barricades, temporary mesh-fencing, and blinding arc lights that cut through the perpetual smog. The air hums with the idling engines of heavy transports and military crawlers.

## History

Before the mass rewrite protocols, Sector Six was a major logistics nexus for the city, connecting the industrial sectors with the outer limits. It was abandoned when the city consolidated its borders, falling into disrepair until Director Vane selected it as the staging point for her mobile rewrite protocol deployment due to its strategic position bordering the Outskirts.

## Culture & Customs

The staging ground operates under strict, procedural martial law. Efficiency and compliance are the only recognized behaviors. Personnel move with precise, clipped purpose, communicating largely through closed tactical networks rather than spoken word.

## Notable Features

- **The Central Distribution Hub**: The primary fortified structure at the center of the yard, acting as the main command node and housing the central signal repeater.
- **The Vehicle Yard**: A sprawling lot filled with heavy transports, crawlers, and supply crates.
- **Perimeter Barricades**: Reinforced ferrocrete walls lined with automated sentry turrets and mesh sensors.

## Current State

The staging ground is fully operational and heavily fortified. It is currently marshaling heavy transports equipped with signal repeaters, preparing to deploy them in a perimeter around the Scrapyard Settlement to execute a localized, concentrated memory wipe.
