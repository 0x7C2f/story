# Sector Six Staging Ground

## Description

A sprawling, utilitarian compound carved out of a former logistics distribution hub on the edge of the Dead Zone. The area is dominated by rusted corrugated metal, cracked ferrocrete, and heavy barricades. Harsh halogen floodlights cut through the ever-present haze of the Outskirts, illuminating orderly rows of parked heavy transports.

## History

Originally built to manage freight and supplies during the city's earlier, more expansionist days, the hub was abandoned when the Dead Zone expanded and rendered the routes unviable. The Oversight Committee recently reclaimed it to serve as a discreet, heavily fortified marshaling point for their loyalist forces.

## Culture & Customs

There is no native culture here, only the rigid, methodical discipline of Committee enforcers and technicians preparing for a military operation. Paranoia is high, with strict authentication protocols and constant patrols.

## Notable Features

- **The Perimeter Barricades:** Heavy concrete blocks and reinforced boom gates that choke off the old highway leading into the staging ground.
- **The Central Bunker:** A heavily fortified structure in the center of the yard, formerly an administrative office, now reinforced with durasteel plating. It houses the primary signal repeater—the central node of the mobile rewrite network—distinguished by the massive dish antenna on its roof.
- **The Fuel Silos:** Large, rusted tanks on the far edge of the yard, repurposed by the Committee to fuel their crawler fleet.

## Current State

The staging ground is currently active, serving as the launch point for Director Vane's mobile rewrite protocol. It has just been infiltrated by the resistance, and a major explosion at the fuel depot has triggered a facility-wide alert.
