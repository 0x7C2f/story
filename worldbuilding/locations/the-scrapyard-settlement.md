# The Scrapyard Settlement

## Description
A sprawling, makeshift fortress situated in the Outskirts beyond the city grid of New Providence. Constructed from the skeletal remains of old transit trains, rusted derelicts, and reinforced with corrugated steel plates. It stands as a stark contrast to the sterile precision of the city.

## Features
- **Repurposed Airlock Gate:** A heavily reinforced main entrance built from old industrial components.
- **Fungal Farms:** Agricultural areas within the settlement to provide food, fueled by scavenged organic matter.
- **Makeshift Defenses:** Guard posts established in overturned haulers and old transit cars.

## History
Built by outcasts, exiles, and dissidents who survived the harsh conditions of the Outskirts. It serves as the primary base for the Scavengers faction.
