# Sector Five

## Description

Sector Five is a sprawling industrial zone dominated by atmospheric filtration towers and chemical processing plants. A permanent haze hangs over the district, painting the sky in sickly hues of ochre and gray. The architecture is utilitarian and oppressive, composed of reinforced concrete blocks and rust-stained gantries. The air tastes of ozone and sulfur.

## History

Originally constructed as the primary environmental control hub for New Providence, Sector Five gradually absorbed the displaced working-class population as the inner sectors expanded. It has always been a hotbed for minor labor disputes, which the Oversight Committee ruthlessly suppressed through strategic memory alterations.

## Culture & Customs

The residents of Sector Five live by a strict code of silence and communal reliance. Shifts at the filtration plants dictate the rhythm of life, and unofficial syndicates manage the black market for respiratory gear and clean water. Suspicion of Committee authorities is high, though rarely vocalized out of fear of being selected for "re-alignment."

## Notable Features

- **The Filtration Stacks:** Massive towers that dominate the skyline, constantly venting processed atmosphere.
- **The Grinders:** The local colloquialism for the industrial processing lines where most residents work.
- **Sub-Level Access Nodes:** Numerous maintenance hatches that connect to the older smuggler tunnels beneath the city.

## Current State

Sector Five is in the early stages of a violent awakening. The mesh network broadcast has bypassed the local jammers, flooding the workers' terminals with unaltered memories of suppressed strikes and vanished colleagues. The initial confusion is rapidly crystallizing into directed anger against the Oversight Committee.
