# Sector Five

## Description

A sprawling industrial district characterized by colossal coolant towers, heavy manufactories, and a relentless mechanical hum. The air tastes of sulfur and machine oil, and a thick layer of smog perpetually chokes the sky. The architecture is heavy canvas and steel, built for utility rather than comfort.

## History

Sector Five has long been the industrial heart of the lower tiers, churning out the machinery and materials required to keep the city running. It is populated heavily by blue-collar workers subjected to grueling shifts.

## Culture & Customs

The workers here wear heavy canvas jumpsuits and adhere to strict shift schedules dictated by automated assembly lines. Due to the harsh conditions, there is a strong sense of solidarity, though historically suppressed by the Oversight Committee's enforcers.

## Notable Features

- **Coolant Towers:** Massive structures that dominate the skyline, offering shadows and narrow alleys between factory blocs.
- **Transit Plazas:** Wide open areas equipped with public terminals where workers gather during shift changes.

## Current State

Currently in a state of growing unrest. The public terminals are broadcasting the counter-broadcast on a loop, exposing the Oversight Committee's memory alterations. The factory workers are confused and their anger is rapidly rising, on the verge of turning into a full-scale riot.
