# Sector Four

## Description

A massive transit hub serving as a choke point to the inner districts. Defined by its interlocking platforms and magnetic rails.

## History

Once a bustling civilian transport artery, heavily militarized by the Committee after the first protests.

## Culture & Customs

Strict curfews and heavy patrols. The population moves quickly, heads down.

## Notable Features

The Transit Hub, automated gun emplacements.

## Current State

Currently under assault by the resistance.
