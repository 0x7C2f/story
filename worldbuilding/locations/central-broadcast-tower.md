# Central Broadcast Tower

## Description

The central broadcast tower is a monolithic, highly fortified structure situated in the heart of Sector One. It stands as a dark spire against the skyline, bristling with communication arrays, hardened transmission lines, and encrypted satellite uplinks. Functionally, it is the primary communications and broadcast hub for the entire city.

## Purpose and Function

The tower was originally built to ensure reliable communication across all sectors and to coordinate civil defense and public broadcasts. It maintains the highest bandwidth and widest reach of any node in the city's network. Because it controls the primary broadcast channels, it is the only node powerful enough to push massive data overwrites—or counter-broadcasts—across the entire grid simultaneously, circumventing local sub-network bottlenecks.

## Defenses and Access

As a critical piece of infrastructure, the tower is heavily guarded by the Oversight Committee's security forces. Access is strictly compartmentalized, requiring high-level clearances to reach the upper transmission decks. The physical control terminals are isolated behind multiple layers of security and intentionally air-gapped from external networks to prevent remote tampering. These physical air-gaps necessitate manual intervention for any deep-level system overrides.

## Continuity Notes

- **Role in the Narrative**: The central broadcast tower is the focal point of Elias and Kaelen's operation in Chapter 11. It is the only place where the cryptographic root key can be deployed to push the unaltered memories to the populace before Project: Synchronization completes its mass overwrite.
- **Physical Characteristics**: A dark, imposing spire with bristling antennae, representing the centralized power of the Oversight Committee.
