# Sector Four Alley

## Description

A dingy alley in Sector Four of Neo-Veridia. Constantly slick with rain. Smells of ozone, old synth-spices, and the metallic tang of desperation.

## History

Like most of Sector Four, it has long been neglected by the corporations in the upper levels.

## Culture & Customs

Desperation is the currency here. Illegal trades and black market deals are the norm.

## Notable Features

Features a flickering overhang of a defunct noodle stand where deals are made in the shadows.

## Current State

The location where Elias Thorne just performed a memory extraction on an unknown paranoid client.
