# Nexus Archives

## Description

The Nexus Archives is a massive, monolithic structure located in the heart of New Providence. From the outside, it appears as a windowless, imposing citadel of black synth-stone and glass. Inside, it is a labyrinthine network of dimly lit corridors, humming server clusters, and subterranean vaults. The air always tastes faintly of ozone and synthetic filter dust.

## History

Built shortly after the Digitization Mandate of 2038, the Nexus Archives was established to curate and store the digitized memories of every citizen in New Providence. It was initially pitched as a way to preserve human history with perfect fidelity, immune to the decay of biological memory.

## Culture & Customs

The environment inside the Archives is highly structured and sterile. Archivists like Elias Thorne are expected to adhere to strict protocols, focusing on their terminals and avoiding unnecessary interaction. Curiosity is discouraged; compliance is mandatory.

## Notable Features

- **Archive Nodes:** The workstations where archivists catalog and compress memory files.
- **Sector 4:** A secure, lower-level sector containing highly sensitive or corrupted data, sealed by heavy blast doors during security breaches.
- **Cooling Shafts:** A sprawling network of maintenance and ventilation shafts used to regulate the massive server farms, doubling as an escape route for desperate fugitives.

## Current State

Currently under high alert following an unauthorized data extraction by former archivist Elias Thorne. Sentinels actively patrol the corridors with lethal suppression protocols authorized.
