# Nexus Archives

## Overview

The central repository for digitized human memories in New Providence. It is a massive, sprawling facility located in the upper tiers of the city, characterized by sterile halls, advanced climate control, and overwhelming security.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Gleaming obsidian archive nodes, flickering holographic displays of raw sensory data, stark white and slate-grey architecture.
- **Sound:** The low, omnipresent hum of cooling systems and servers, the sharp chimes of access terminals, echoing footsteps in vast corridors.
- **Smell:** Ozone, synthetic cleaners, cold, filtered air.

## Role in the Story

Elias's workplace and the setting of the inciting incident. It represents the oppressive, sanitized control the Oversight Committee exerts over memory and truth. It is heavily guarded by automated sentinels and corporate enforcers.
