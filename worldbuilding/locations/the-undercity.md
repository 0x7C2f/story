# The Undercity

## Overview

The decaying, lower levels of New Providence, existing entirely beneath the gleaming spires of the upper city. It is a labyrinth of neon-lit alleys, abandoned maintenance sectors, and unregulated data-markets.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Sickly neon signs casting harsh shadows, rusted bulkheads, condensation dripping from exposed pipes, dense crowds of scavengers.
- **Sound:** The shriek of aging transport tubes, the chaotic hum of a thousand unregulated generators, muffled shouts, and the constant drip of water.
- **Smell:** Synthesized rot, stale ozone, damp concrete, and unwashed bodies.

## Role in the Story

A sanctuary for outcasts, rogue data-brokers, and those fleeing the Oversight Committee. It serves as Elias's primary refuge after his escape from the Nexus Archives and the location where he seeks out allies like Kaelen. It is a technological "dead zone" where the corporate monitoring grid struggles to penetrate.
