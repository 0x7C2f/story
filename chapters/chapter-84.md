# Chapter 84

## Outline

1. Elias and his team process the reality of being sealed inside the Archive core.
2. Kaelen and Jarek secure the physical space while Elias interfaces with the core to begin downloading the uncorrupted history.
3. Vane realizes the Archive core's internal systems are detached from the external network, meaning she can't remotely stop the download; she prepares a physical assault force to breach the blast doors.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias stood in the center of the Archive core, the metallic slam of the blast doors still ringing in his ears. The frigid air nipped at his skin, a stark contrast to the burning adrenaline coursing through his veins. They were sealed in.

"Perimeter is clear, but we're sitting ducks if they blow that door," Kaelen reported, her rifle trained on the massive barricade they had just passed through.

Jarek grunted, his heavy frame casting a long shadow across the pulsing memory banks. "They sealed it to keep us in. Means they don't want to destroy what's in here. We have time."

Elias nodded, his procedural training taking over the rising panic. He turned his attention to the central terminal, a colossal interface of glass and rusted metal. He engaged the primary console, his fingers flying across the archaic keys. The system hummed to life, and the raw, uncorrupted history of New Providence began to scroll across the massive screens.

High above in the Nexus Spire, Director Vane analyzed the telemetry. The external network was locked, but the internal system of the core was independent. Elias was already accessing the forbidden files. Her cold, calculated mask cracked. She opened a channel to her tactical command. "Prepare a localized breaching charge for the primary access gate of Sector Alpha. I want a full assault force ready in five minutes."
