# Chapter 48

## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek rally the workers to fall back from Checkpoint Charlie before the Committee's heavy artillery arrives.
2. They retreat deeper into Sector Five, using the dense industrial environment to set up layered choke points and ambush zones.
3. Kaelen intercepts a new transmission confirming the heavy armor is mobilizing, setting a grim deadline for their defensive preparations.

---

## Chapter Text

"Fall back!" Elias shouted, his voice cracking over the din of the transit hub. He waved his arm, signaling the workers behind the barricades. "They are sending heavy armor! We cannot hold this position!"

For a moment, the workers hesitated. The adrenaline of destroying the enforcer drones still fueled them, painting a false picture of invincibility. But Jarek stepped forward, his massive frame imposing even in the dim, dust-choked light.

"You heard him!" Jarek bellowed, pointing his heavy plasma torch toward the rear blast doors. "Move! Unless you want to try punching through a Goliath tank with a rivet gun! We fall back to the foundries!"

That broke the spell. The workers scrambled, abandoning the forward barricades and flooding toward the rear exits. Kaelen fell in beside Elias, her carbine tracking the breach where the enforcer drones had entered.

"They will not take long to realize the recon units are offline," Kaelen said, her eyes scanning the shadows. "When the heavy units arrive, they will level Checkpoint Charlie just to clear the sightlines."

"Then we make sure we are not here," Elias replied. He turned and sprinted after the retreating workers, the heavy thud of his boots echoing against the concrete.

The retreat into Sector Five was chaotic but swift. They rushed through the maze of corridors of the industrial zone, the air growing thick with the smell of ozone and superheated metal. The foundries were a sprawling complex of catwalks, massive smelting vats, and narrow maintenance trenches—a perfect environment for a guerrilla defense.

"Jarek!" Elias called out over the roar of the dormant machinery. "We need to set up layered choke points. We cannot stop heavy armor in a pitched battle, but we can bleed them for every inch of ground."

Jarek nodded, his mind already assessing the tactical layout. "We use the smelting vats. We can rig the coolant lines to flood the lower trenches. It will slow down the treads and blind their thermal sensors. You three," he pointed to a group of workers carrying heavy magnetic charges, "start rigging the primary catwalks. If they try to bypass the trenches, we drop the ceiling on them."

Elias watched the workers spring into action, their fear replaced by grim determination. He turned to Kaelen. She had her wrist terminal deployed, her fingers flying across the holographic interface.

"What do you have?" Elias asked, dreading the answer.

"I have tapped into their local tactical net again," Kaelen said, her voice tight. "It is not good. They are not just sending armor. Vane has authorized a full quarantine protocol. They are sealing the blast doors at the sector perimeter."

Elias felt a cold knot form in his stomach. "They are trapping us in here."

"Worse," Kaelen corrected. She looked up, the glow of the terminal reflecting in her eyes. "They are mobilizing two Goliath-class assault frames. They are going to raze the foundries until there is nothing left but slag."
