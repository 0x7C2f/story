## Chapter Text

Director Vane watched the signal bleed across the sector map and felt, for the first time in decades, the ground shift beneath her. The Committee's grid stuttered, spitting malformed data back at her analysts. Archivist Thorne had done exactly what the models said could not be done.

She terminated the override sequence and stood. The room felt smaller than it had that morning. There would be consequences—for her, for the Committee—but the louder question now was how much of the city had already heard. She picked up her coat and stepped toward the door without looking back at the darkening screens.
