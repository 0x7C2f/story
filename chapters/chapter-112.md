# Chapter 112: The Dawn of Truth

## Outline

1. The network override successfully broadcasts the uncorrupted history across the entire mesh.
2. Vane's synchronization feed crumbles, throwing her enforcers into chaotic disarray.
3. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek use the confusion to slip past the disoriented Committee forces and escape into the deeper Undercity.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias slumped against the vibrating metal console, his breathing ragged and shallow. The small, cramped bunker felt suddenly cavernous as the echoing gunfire outside ceased abruptly. He watched the terminal screen with wide, unbelieving eyes. The progress bar for the network override had vanished, replaced by a single, steady pulse of brilliant white light. The uncorrupted history was loose. It was flowing through the city's mesh like a digital flood, overwhelming the carefully constructed barriers of the Committee's synchronization feed.

Kaelen lowered her smoking sidearms. She peered cautiously around the shattered remnants of the blast door. "They have stopped shooting," she whispered, her voice tight with confusion. "They are just standing there."

Jarek pushed past her, his heavy slug-thrower still raised. The enforcers in the corridor were frozen, their glowing visors flickering erratically between aggressive red and confused static. Some staggered, clutching their helmets as the overwhelming influx of truth clashed violently with their programmed directives. The systemic failure of Vane's control mechanisms was sudden and catastrophic, leaving her elite forces paralyzed by the sheer volume of unfiltered memory data.

"The feed is broken," Elias said. His voice was hoarse, but a profound sense of relief washed over him. "Vane's grip is shattered. They do not know what their orders are anymore."

"We need to move," Kaelen urged, snapping out of her stunned silence. "This confusion will not last forever. We have a narrow window to vanish before they reboot or resort to manual overrides."

Elias nodded slowly. He detached his neural rig from the console with shaking hands. The physical toll of the hack was immense, but the victory tasted sweeter than any physical pain. They slipped past the disoriented enforcers, moving silently through the smoky corridor and descending further into the labyrinthine depths of the Undercity. Behind them, the Committee's iron grip on the citizens finally fractured, leaving room for a new, uncertain dawn.
