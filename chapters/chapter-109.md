# Chapter 109

## Outline

## Chapter Text

Elias Thorne traced the seam of the heavy iron door with numb fingers, feeling the chill of the Undercity seep through his worn jacket. The reinforced bunker offered a temporary reprieve, but the silence pressing against the steel felt heavy, pregnant with the threat of discovery. He adjusted the strap of his rig, the familiar weight a small comfort against his bruised ribs.

Kaelen crouched near the center of the room, carefully cleaning a scavenged kinetic pistol by the dim light of a cracked glow-tube. She checked the firing mechanism, her movements precise, almost rhythmic. "They are close," she said, her voice dropping to a harsh whisper. "The sweep teams. I heard the distinct rhythm of their marching patterns before we sealed the hatch."

Jarek grunted, shifting his massive frame against a rusted support beam. He tested the heft of a salvaged iron pipe, his scarred face unreadable in the gloom. "Let them come," he rumbled, the word 'come' lingering like a challenge. "This bunker is a bottleneck. We can hold the entryway."

Elias turned away from the door, his eyes scanning the debris-strewn floor. Near the back wall, half-buried under a collapsed ventilation duct, sat a dust-covered console. It was an archaic model, pre-Committee, entirely decoupled from the Spire's central network. He knelt beside it, brushing away the grime to reveal a tactile interface.

He connected his rig to the console's auxiliary port, bypassing the rusted physical locks. A flicker of amber light danced across the cracked glass, revealing lines of raw, localized telemetry. It was a closed loop, monitoring the immediate physical perimeter.

The screen resolved into a crude tactical map. Three distinct clusters of thermal signatures were converging on their coordinates. Vane's analogue sweep teams were moving with terrifying efficiency, closing the net.

"They have found us," Elias said quietly, disconnecting his rig as the first heavy thud echoed against the outer blast door.
