## Chapter Text

The physical data trunks running beneath the Sector Four black site were housed in a reinforced subterranean vault. Elias Thorne tightened his grip on the rusted crowbar, ignoring the protests of his wounded ribs as he stared at the heavy blast doors. Kaelen was already working the access panel, her cybernetic arm a blur of motion.

"The local security subroutines are locked behind her root clearance," Kaelen reported. "I am trying to force a handshake protocol, but the encryption is shifting."

Jarek racked his heavy rifle. "Then we don't ask politely."

Before Kaelen could protest, Jarek wedged a breaching charge into the door seam and shoved them back. The detonation was a localized thunderclap that tore the blast doors off their hinges. Elias pushed through the settling dust, coughing. The vault was massive, filled with rows of humming data trunks the size of ancient sequoias.

High above in the Spire, Director Vane watched the breach on her tactical displays. "They are targeting the physical infrastructure," she said, her voice an administrative monotone. "Deploy the heavy sentries to the vault. Terminate them."
