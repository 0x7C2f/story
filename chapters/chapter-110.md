## Chapter Text

Elias pushed through the crowded thoroughfare of the Undercity, the air thick with tension and the smell of ozone. They had to finish this. The liberation of their memories was finally within their grasp. He gripped the data drive tightly in his pocket. It felt heavier than it should have, burdened by the weight of a thousand forgotten lives.

He glanced at the terminal screens lining the rusted walls. A low static hum vibrated through the metal grating beneath his boots. The network was primed, waiting for his signal. This was the moment they had been planning for months.

"Are we ready?" Kaela's voice crackled in his earpiece, sharp and clear despite the interference.
"Almost," Elias replied, picking up his pace. "We just need to hit the primary node."

Suddenly, the overhead lights flickered and died, plunging the corridor into darkness. A heavy mechanical thud echoed from the upper levels. Vane's forces were here. They had found them faster than anticipated. Elias drew a sharp breath, his hand instinctively reaching for the blaster at his side. The real fight was about to begin.
