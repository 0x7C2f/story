# Elias at The Undercity - Phase 78
## Outline

1. Elias attempts to interface with the dormant terminal in the pre-Committee transit station, using an improvised connection from his remaining gear.
2. The terminal boots up, revealing an isolated, secondary data-line directly into the Nexus Archives, but triggers a localized power surge.
3. The surge alerts Vane's surviving hunter-killer subsystems, forcing Kaelen and Jarek to prepare a hasty defense at the station's perimeter while Elias races to establish the uplink.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias knelt before the dust-caked terminal, his hands moving with practiced precision despite the tremor in his fingers. The bulky console was a relic, a piece of pre-Committee hardware that belonged in a museum rather than the depths of the unmapped Undercity. He pulled a frayed connector cable from his pack, stripping the shielding with a quick bite of his teeth, and spliced the raw copper into the terminal's exposed port.

"You really think this artifact can cut through the interference?" Kaelen asked, her voice a tight whisper as she paced the edge of the platform, rifle sweeping the darkness.

"It's not about cutting through," Elias said, twisting the wires together. "It's about going under. This station predates the mesh network. If the hardline is still intact, it might bypass Vane's quarantine entirely."

Jarek grunted from the turnstiles, adjusting a scavenged proximity mine against a rusted pillar. "If it works. If it does not, we are sitting on a dead end."

Elias exhaled, ignoring the throb in his knee, and routed power from his emergency battery cell into the splice. For a long second, nothing happened. Then, a low, mechanical hum vibrated through the cracked ceramic tiles beneath them. The terminal's screen flickered to life, a sickly amber glow illuminating the dust dancing in the air. Lines of archaic code spilled across the monitor.

Elias leaned closer, his eyes scanning the data stream. "We have a handshake," he breathed. "It's an isolated line. Direct to the Nexus Archives."

Before he could type the first command, a sharp crack echoed through the cavernous space. The amber light flared violently, and a localized power surge ripped through the station's dormant lighting grid. Banks of ancient fluorescents snapped on with a blinding flash, illuminating the skeletal transit car and the sprawling platform before immediately shattering in a shower of sparks.

"Elias!" Kaelen barked, dropping into a crouch. "Tell me that was you."

"A power feedback loop," Elias said, his hands flying across the terminal's clunky keyboard. "The surge... it's going to ping every sensor on the grid."

From the darkness of the tunnel they had just traversed, a high-pitched mechanical whine began to build, echoing off the plasteel archways.

"Hunter subsystems," Jarek rumbled, raising his heavy repeater. "They found the spike."

"Hold them off," Elias said, his eyes locked on the amber text. "Just give me three minutes to establish the uplink."
