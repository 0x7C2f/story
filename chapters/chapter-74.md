# Chapter 74

## Outline

1. Loyalist enforcers pour through the breached blast door, initiating a fierce firefight.
2. Jarek provides suppressing fire with his heavy rifle, allowing Elias to detach his neural rig from the terminal.
3. Kaelen blows out the floor grating, and the trio escapes into the dark, collapsed maintenance shaft below as the Enforcers secure the room.

## Chapter Text

The heavy kinetics of the Loyalist assault rifles drowned out the terminal's hum. Ceramic armor plates scraped against the ruined blast door as the enforcers poured into the narrow space, their targeting lasers cutting tight red arcs through the smoke.

Elias flinched as a ricochet kissed the chassis of the main router, showering sparks over his arms. The synchronization bar on his wrist terminal held steady at one hundred percent, but the physical connection to the neural rig remained tethered. If he ripped the cord without running the disconnect protocol, the backlash would fry the terminal and likely his own cortex with it.

"I need ten seconds!" Elias shouted, his voice barely audible over the gunfire.

"You have five!" Kaelen fired twice, precise and ruthless, dropping the lead enforcer. She did not check the body; she was already shifting her aim to the gap.

Jarek did not bother counting. The Scavenger stepped out from the cover of a fractured concrete pillar, hauling his heavy rifle up to his shoulder. He did not aim so much as sweep the breach, firing a deafening, sustained volley that forced the Loyalist squad to throw themselves against the outer corridor walls. The vibration shook dust from the ceiling and rattled the teeth in Elias's skull.

Elias hit the execute command for the disconnect. The interface decoupled with a sharp, pneumatic hiss. He ripped the tether from his temple, the sudden loss of connection hitting him with a wave of intense nausea. He staggered, grasping the edge of the console to steady himself.

"It's done. The broadcast is holding," Elias breathed, his chest heaving.

"Then we don't hold the room," Kaelen stated. She slapped a shaped charge against the rusted floor grating near the rear ventilation housing and hit the detonator.

The confined explosion blew the metal panel into the dark, revealing the jagged throat of a collapsed maintenance shaft. Cold, stale air rushed upward, carrying the smell of ancient rot and stagnant water.

Jarek reloaded his rifle, keeping his eyes on the corridor. "Go. They are bringing heavy armor next."

Elias did not argue. He swung his legs over the edge of the hole, gripping the decaying rungs of the ladder, and descended into the pitch black. Kaelen followed a second later.

Above them, Jarek fired one last, echoing burst before dropping into the shaft. The heavy thud of enforcer boots hit the floorboards of the substation just as the darkness swallowed them.

Elias checked his wrist terminal. The blue light of the counter-signal pulsed steadily in the gloom, but the echo of pursuing footfalls vibrated through the metal ladder. Director Vane's hunters were not stopping at the breach.
