## Chapter Text

Elias Thorne stood at the apex of the Nexus Spire with the root key burning in his palm and the city spread below like a circuit board waiting for new instructions. Below, Vane's last loyalist transport was a wreck of blackened steel. The mesh network held. The truth was already moving.

He checked the console one last time. Signal active. No override queued. The Oversight Committee's crown jewel—the broadcast tower—was now a public channel, its relays forced to carry facts instead of sterilized fictions. For the first time in months, Elias Thorne knew what the city sounded like when it was not being lied to.

He pocketed the root key and turned his back on the receiver. There would be retaliation. There always was. But the archive was open now, and millions of citizens had just woken up with the pieces of their own lives in front of them. The work of rebuilding truth belonged to all of them.

He stepped into the lift and pressed the key to descend.
