# Chapter 32

## Outline

1. The heavy transport bluff fails as the enforcer manual inspection detects Elias and Kaelen.
2. A brief, violent skirmish ensues at the security checkpoint, forcing Kaelen to use the transport's reinforced hull to ram the barricade.
3. They breach the perimeter but must abandon the transport, slipping into the Nexus Spire's lower ventilation shafts as the alarm blares.

---

## Chapter Text

The heavy transport idled, its massive engine block vibrating against the ferrocrete as the Committee enforcer approached. Elias watched the diagnostic feed, his pulse hammering against his ribs. The manual check wasn't supposed to happen. The spoofed transponder had cleared the automated turrets, but the human element remained unpredictable.

Kaelen shifted in the pilot's seat, her hand dropping from the control yoke to the heavy pulse pistol strapped to her thigh. "If he opens that door, we have less than a second," she murmured, her voice clipped and cold.

Elias uncoupled the neural rig from his temple. The sudden absence of the chaotic network chatter left a ringing silence in his ears. Outside, the enforcer tapped a gauntleted hand against the reinforced door, holding up a portable scanner.

"Authorization codes," the enforcer shouted, the voice muffled through the armor plating.

Elias met Kaelen's eyes. There was no authorization code that would hold up to a manual, biometric scan. The bluff was over.

"Brace," Kaelen said.

She didn't wait for the enforcer to realize the discrepancy. With a fluid, brutal motion, she slammed the transport into drive and floored the accelerator. The heavy vehicle surged forward, a multi-ton battering ram of steel and armor. The enforcer was clipped by the reinforced bumper, sent spinning into the adjacent barricade with a sickening crunch.

Alarms immediately shrieked across the checkpoint. The inactive turrets swiveled, their tracking lasers painting the hull in frantic streaks of crimson. Plasma bolts began to scorch the ferrocrete, superheated air hissing as the shots grazed the heavy transport's side.

"They're painting us!" Elias yelled over the roar of the engine.

"I know," Kaelen snarled, wrestling the yoke as the transport careened toward the secondary barricade. "Hold on."

The impact was deafening. The transport slammed into the plasteel barrier, tearing it from its moorings. The windshield spider-webbed, alarms blaring inside the cabin as the vehicle's structural integrity warnings flashed red. They punched through the checkpoint, skidding onto the polished transit lanes that fed directly into the lower levels of the Nexus Spire.

"The engine is redlining," Elias said, reading the fractured diagnostic screen. "We ruptured the primary coolant line on the barrier."

"It got us through," Kaelen replied, wrenching the transport into a sharp turn down a service access corridor, away from the main thoroughfare. Smoke began to pour through the ventilation vents, filling the cabin with the acrid scent of burning synthetic fluids. "But it won't get us much further."

She slammed the brakes, throwing them both forward as the transport skidded to a halt in a shadowed loading bay at the base of the Spire. The massive structure loomed above them, a monolithic tomb of secrets.

"Out," Kaelen ordered, already popping her door seal. "They'll have response teams here in ninety seconds."

Elias grabbed his gear pack and scrambled out of the smoking transport. The loading bay was deserted, smelling of chemical cleaner and ozone. High above them, the wailing siren of the Spire's internal security system echoed down the reinforced shafts.

"The main lifts are locked down," Elias said, his mind racing through the schematics he had memorized. "But the lower ventilation shafts connect directly to the sub-basement infrastructure. Vane won't expect an approach from the industrial cyclers."

"Then we go under," Kaelen said, checking the charge on her pistol. She cast a quick glance back at the transport, then at the sprawling complex of the Spire. "Jarek bought us the opening. Now we have to use it."

They slipped into the shadows, moving toward the heavy grated vents that lined the base of the fortress, leaving the smoking transport behind as the first sounds of approaching enforcer boots echoed in the distance.
