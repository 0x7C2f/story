# Elias at Sector Four - Phase 68
## Outline

1. Elias realizes he must detach the tracker from his deck to misdirect Director Vane's Heavy Enforcers.
2. Jarek and Kaelen defend a narrow maintenance shaft choke point against an advancing scout team to buy Elias time.
3. Elias successfully rigs the power management loop of the deck containing the tracker onto a passing automated maintenance drone, leading the Enforcers away and securing their escape.

---

## Chapter Text

The heavy thud of enforcer armor echoed through the ferrocrete arteries of the undercity, vibrating against the soles of Elias's boots. He knelt in the oily runoff of a defunct transit line, his fingers slipping on the casing of his interface deck. The digital anchor Director Vane had planted within the deck's power management loop thrummed like a trapped wasp, broadcasting their location to the approaching strike team.

"They are fifty meters out," Kaelen said, her voice a sharp hiss over the ambient hum of the transit conduit. She crouched behind a rusted support pillar, her submachine gun leveled at the dark corridor ahead.

Jarek braced himself against the opposite wall, his massive slug-thrower resting securely in the crook of his arm. "Let them come," he rumbled. "This corridor is narrow. I will fill it with lead."

Elias focused on the delicate circuitry exposed beneath the deck's cracked chassis. He couldn't just scrub the anchor without bricking the hardware he desperately needed, but he could isolate the specific power loop and detach it. His hands trembled as he rerouted the micro-kernel.

"I need two minutes," Elias muttered, his breath hitching.

"You have one," Kaelen replied, not looking away from her sights.

A shadow shifted in the gloom. The sharp crack of Kaelen's weapon shattered the silence, followed instantly by the deafening roar of Jarek's slug-thrower. The scout team had arrived. Return fire chewed into the ferrocrete around them, raining dust down on Elias's shoulders. He forced himself to ignore the chaos, tracing the power lines to the localized battery housing the anchor.

A rusted, automated maintenance drone clicked past him on its segmented treads, ignoring the firefight in favor of sweeping debris.

Elias grabbed the drone's housing, forcing it to halt. With a frantic twist of his field-splicer, he severed the localized battery from his deck and fused the raw connection points to the drone's primary sensor array. The anchor transferred, its signal pulsing through the drone's architecture.

"Done!" Elias shouted, scrambling back as the drone trundled forward, turning down a secondary access tunnel away from their position.

"Move!" Jarek roared, providing covering fire as Elias and Kaelen sprinted into the opposite maintenance shaft. The heavy thud of the Enforcer squad shifted course, chasing the bright, undeniable signal of the maintenance drone into the depths of the undercity.
