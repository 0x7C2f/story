# Chapter 100: The Archivist's Stand

## Outline

1. Opening beat: Enforcers assault the hub door as Elias monitors the upload.
2. Escalation: Kaelen and Jarek create a distraction outside, but a heavy sentry breaches the door.
3. Turn or decision: Elias makes a desperate choice to overload the primary relay to destroy the heavy sentry, completing the broadcast but trapping himself.

---

## Chapter Text

The heavy blast door groaned under the sustained impact of plasma fire. The central distribution hub shook, dust raining from the towering server racks, but the thick ferrocrete held—for now. Elias Thorne leaned heavily against the primary control console, his left arm useless, clutching his wounded shoulder. The scent of burned ozone mixed with the copper tang of his own blood.

*Upload Progress: 42%.*

The pulsing blue light of the data streams bathed the room in an unnatural glow. Outside, the chaos of the staging ground roared. The deep thrum of heavy enforcer transports reverberated through the floorboards. Director Vane's forces were massing, pinpointing his location with terrifying efficiency. He checked the neural tether connecting him to the hub's mainframe; the connection was stable, but raw feedback spiked pain into his skull with every cycle.

"Elias!" Kaelen's voice crackled over the comms, sharp and urgent. "They are bringing up a siege crawler. We cannot hold the perimeter!"

"Buy me three minutes," Elias gasped, his voice tight. "The mesh network is saturating. If they cut the hardline now, the localized repeaters will still catch the signal."

"We do not have three minutes," Jarek grunted over the channel, the sound of heavy kinetic fire echoing behind him. "I am setting charges on the eastern barricade to flank them, but the crawler is turning its main cannon on your door."

Elias watched the progress bar inch forward. *48%.* He knew Vane's tactics. She wouldn't just bomb the hub; she needed to secure the primary relay to ensure the counter-broadcast hadn't corrupted her own core systems. She would send in a heavy sentry unit first.

A deafening crash shattered the relative quiet of the hub. The center of the blast door bowed inward, glowing white-hot. A second impact tore a jagged hole in the metal. Through the smoke and flying shrapnel, the hulking silhouette of a Committee heavy sentry stepped into the breach, its rotary cannon spinning up with a high-pitched whine.

Elias drew his sidearm with his right hand, knowing it was useless against the sentry's heavy armor. He glanced at the console. The primary relay hummed directly beneath the blast door.

*Upload Progress: 61%.*

If the sentry destroyed the console, the broadcast failed. Elias made the only calculation left to him. He overrode the safety protocols on the primary relay, routing the entire power output of the hub into a feedback loop.

"Kaelen, Jarek," Elias yelled into the comms, bracing himself behind the console. "Get clear!"

The sentry raised its weapon. Elias slammed his fist onto the execution command, detonating the relay in a blinding flash of raw energy.
