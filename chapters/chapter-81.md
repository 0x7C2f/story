## Chapter Text

Elias led the descent into the thermal exhaust port, the metal rungs searing even through his reinforced gloves. Below them, the darkness swallowed the narrow shaft, broken only by the sporadic, pulsing glow of active heat sinks further down in Quarantine Sector Alpha. Behind him, Kaelen's breathing was ragged but steady, a testament to her sheer endurance, while Jarek brought up the rear, his massive frame barely squeezing through the structural reinforcements. Above them, the dull thud of heavy boots vibrating through the plasteel indicated Vane's forces were already locking down the perimeter they had just left behind.
