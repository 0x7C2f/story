# Elias at Quarantine Sector Alpha - Phase 83
## Outline

1. Elias overrides the local network counter-measure, granting the team deeper access into the Archive core.
2. Vane monitors the intrusion from her command center, realizing they breached her trap, and opts to seal the physical blast doors to the core.
3. The team hears the heavy blast doors slam shut, realizing they are trapped inside with the truth but no way out.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias keyed a final sequence into the rusted console, holding his breath as the red warning screen flickered. The digital counter-measure dissolved into a cascade of green acceptance runes, unlocking the heavy primary access gate ahead of them. He exhaled sharply, wiping sweat from his brow, as the rusted servos whined and the massive doors groaned open to reveal the Archive core.

Inside, the air grew instantly frigid, preserved perfectly by backup cooling systems. Rows of glass-enclosed memory banks stretched into the abyss, pulsing with a faint, ghostly light. Kaelen raised her rifle, sweeping the shadows with practiced precision, while Jarek pushed past to secure the immediate perimeter.

Deep inside the Nexus Spire, Director Vane watched the telemetry shift on her command console, her expression hardening into a rigid mask of calculated fury. Elias Thorne had bypassed her trap, entering the one place she had forbidden. She tapped the master override switch, bypassing standard security protocols.

A deafening metallic slam echoed through the Archive core, shaking the very floor beneath Elias's boots. The heavy physical blast doors at the entrance sealed shut behind them, the locking mechanisms engaging with a definitive, inescapable finality. Elias turned slowly, staring at the barricade that now trapped them with the city's stolen history.
