# Elias at Quarantine Sector Alpha - Phase 82
## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek clear the exhaust port and enter the main facility of Quarantine Sector Alpha.
2. The team evades a patrol of loyalist enforcers searching the upper catwalks.
3. Elias finds a hidden maintenance terminal and slices into the local network to locate the Archive core, only to discover an active counter-measure left by Director Vane.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias dropped from the scorched exhaust grate, his boots hitting the grated floor of Quarantine Sector Alpha with a heavy, metallic clang. Above him, Kaelen slid down gracefully, her dark combat armor blending into the shadows, followed a moment later by the heavy thump of Jarek's landing.

"Watch the crossways," Elias whispered, checking the charge on his datapad. The vast, cavernous space hummed with ancient, dormant machinery. Rust covered the bulkheads, and the air smelled of ozone and stale copper. They moved swiftly through the gloom.

Suddenly, the staccato rhythm of heavy boots echoed from the upper catwalks. A squad of loyalist enforcers, carrying high-powered pulse rifles, swept their searchlights across the lower deck. The beam of light narrowly missed Kaelen as she pressed herself against a rusted pillar.

Elias motioned toward a side corridor. They slipped away, navigating the labyrinthine passages until Elias spotted a flickering amber light on a rusted wall panel. He pried it open to reveal a hidden maintenance terminal. Quickly, he spliced his datapad into the frayed wires, his fingers dancing across the screen as he attempted to locate the Archive core. A sudden red warning flashed across his display.

"Damn," Elias muttered. "Vane left a counter-measure in the local network. We have to move fast."
