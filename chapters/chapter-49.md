# Elias at Sector Five - Phase 49
## Outline

1. The Goliath-class assault frames breach the outer perimeter of the foundry complex, their heavy weapons tearing through the reinforced walls.
2. Elias and Jarek coordinate the detonation of the rigged coolant lines, flooding the lower trenches and stalling the massive tanks.
3. Amid the chaos, Kaelen intercepts an encrypted signal from the Goliaths indicating they carry a payload meant to sever Sector Five's physical connection to the rest of the city, not just destroy it.

---

## Chapter Text

The foundry floor vibrated before the noise hit them. The low, subsonic rumble of the Goliath-class assault frames traveled through the steel decking, a promise of destruction moving at a crawl. Elias gripped the edge of a catwalk railing, peering into the smoke-choked expanse below.

"They are taking the main thoroughfare," Kaelen reported, her wrist terminal casting a harsh blue light over her face. "They are not trying to drive down the side streets. They are just driving through the buildings."

As she spoke, the reinforced exterior wall of the smelting level exploded inward. Slabs of concrete the size of cargo transports tumbled onto the floor, followed by the jagged, armored prow of the first Goliath. It was a rolling fortress of dark composite plating and sloped angles, designed to deflect anything short of an orbital strike. The primary cannon pivoted on its massive turret, the barrel glowing with latent thermal energy.

"Jarek!" Elias shouted over the roar of the engines. "Are the coolant lines ready?"

Below them, Jarek was a silhouette against the glow of the dormant furnaces. He raised a heavy remote detonator. "Ready on your mark!"

The Goliath lurched forward, its treads grinding over the rubble of the outer wall. A secondary turret on its flank opened fire, sweeping a heavy kinetic beam across the lower platforms. Scaffolding vaporized. A group of resistance fighters scrambled for cover behind a line of heavy slag carts.

"Wait for it," Elias said, his voice tight. "Let it cross the trench line."

The massive frame ground forward, its sheer weight cracking the ferrocrete floor. It reached the designated kill box, a recessed area used for transferring molten material.

"Now!" Elias yelled.

Jarek slammed his fist onto the detonator.

A series of muffled detonations ripped through the lower levels. The massive coolant lines, rigged with industrial explosives, ruptured simultaneously. A torrent of super-chilled liquid surged into the trenches directly beneath the Goliath. The sudden, violent temperature shift hit the tank's overheated tread assemblies and the ambient heat of the foundry.

The resulting steam explosion was instantaneous and deafening. A geyser of vapor and shattered rock erupted upward, engulfing the assault frame. The Goliath listed heavily to port as the floor beneath its left tread track collapsed into the flooded trench. Its engines whined in protest, fighting for traction on the slick, compromised surface.

"It is stuck!" Jarek roared, pumping a fist in the air. "Pour it on them!"

From the catwalks and upper gantries, the workers rained fire down on the immobilized tank. Armor-piercing rivet guns, modified plasma cutters, and makeshift explosives battered the sloped armor.

Elias watched the assault, knowing it was not enough. They were chipping paint. The Goliath's main cannon swiveled, searching for a target through the blinding steam.

Kaelen cursed beside him, tapping her terminal. "Elias, I have tapped the tank's short-range telemetry."

"Are they calling for backup?" Elias asked, tracking the second Goliath as it appeared in the breach, cautious of the trap that had caught its leader.

"No," Kaelen said, her eyes widening. "I am reading their ordnance manifests. They are not carrying standard high-explosive or incendiary rounds in the main tubes."

Elias turned to her, the heat of the foundry suddenly cold against his skin. "What are they carrying?"

"Seismic charges," Kaelen replied, her voice dropping. "Deep-core penetrators. Vane is not trying to clear Sector Five. She is going to crack the foundation pillars."

Elias looked down at the massive war machines. They were not here to kill the resistance. They were here to drop the entire sector into the undercity.
