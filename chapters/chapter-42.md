# Elias at New Providence Outskirts - Phase 42
## Outline

1. Elias executes the decentralized mesh network broadcast. The fragmented connections lock into place, surging outward to bypass the perimeter limiters.
2. Committee interceptors sweep into the Outskirts canyon and launch a devastating strafing run on the exposed transport.
3. The heavy transport is destroyed in the attack, forcing Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek to abandon the burning wreckage and flee on foot into the hazardous Outskirts while the automated broadcast continues to propagate.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias Thorne pressed the execution key. The heavy transport's auxiliary terminal froze for a agonizing second before a cascade of green telemetry flooded the screen. The initial, hesitant pings from the Sector Four nodes exploded into a web of synchronous handshakes.

"The mesh is locked," Elias said, his voice tight over the mounting drone of the incoming Committee interceptors. "We are broadcasting."

Outside, Jarek dropped through the open roof hatch, his heavy boots clanging against the deck plates. The scent of scorched plasteel and ozone followed him into the cramped cabin. "They have a firing solution," the scavenger leader grunted, grabbing a heavy pulse rifle from the weapons rack. "Thermal sweeps painted us the moment you lit up the array."

Kaelen braced against the bulkhead, her carbine leveled at the shattered viewport. "How long until the network is self-sustaining?"

"It is already propagating," Elias replied, watching the data stream replicate across hundreds of hijacked nodes. "Every terminal, every comm relay in the lower sectors is echoing the signal. Even if they destroy us, the broadcast will continue until Vane physically cuts the grid."

The first plasma bolt struck the canyon wall fifty meters above them, a blinding flash of blue-white energy that vaporized tons of rusted rock. The resulting shockwave slammed into the transport, tossing Elias against the navigation console. His skull cracked against the hardened display, pain flaring behind his eyes.

"We are sitting ducks in this trench," Jarek roared over the deafening avalanche of falling debris. "We have to move!"

"The transport's armor is gone," Kaelen shouted, her eyes fixed on the darkening sky. "One direct hit will breach the core."

A second interceptor screamed down the canyon, its undercarriage bristling with weapon pods. The transport's threat-warning system screamed in a high, continuous tone.

"Out!" Jarek ordered, kicking the side access door open. The rusted hinges sheared off, sending the heavy door tumbling into the dry wash below. "Everyone out, now!"

Kaelen dove through the opening, hitting the uneven ground in a tight roll. She came up firing, her carbine spitting short, controlled bursts toward the canyon ridge, forcing the interceptor to slightly adjust its angle of attack.

Elias uncoupled his wrist terminal from the main suite, desperate to maintain a link to the mesh. He scrambled toward the exit, the air inside the cabin suddenly searing hot as the interceptor's targeting lasers swept across the exposed chassis.

He launched himself through the door just as a twin volley of plasma rounds slammed into the transport. The impact was deafening. The vehicle lurched violently, the port side caving inward under the immense heat and pressure. The primary drive core whined in protest, a sickly, high-pitched scream that ended in a concussive blast.

Elias hit the ground hard, the wind knocked from his lungs. Jarek grabbed him by the collar, hauling him behind a jagged outcrop of fallen rock as a fireball consumed the heavy transport. The shockwave rolled over them, carrying a wave of blistering heat and raining shrapnel across the canyon floor.

Elias gasped for air, his vision swimming. The transport was gone, reduced to a twisted, burning skeleton of blackened metal. The communication array, their only tether to the network they had just ignited, was slag.

"Stay down," Kaelen ordered, crouching beside them. The interceptors banked sharply, the roar of their engines fading as they circled for another pass. "They will switch to infrared sweeps now that the primary target is neutralized."

Jarek checked the charge on his pulse rifle, his jaw set in a hard, grim line. "We are on foot in the deep Outskirts. No armor, no comms, and every loyalist sweep team in the sector is converging on this canyon."

Elias looked down at his wrist terminal. The screen was cracked, but the pale blue light still flickered. The data stream was chaotic, but it was moving. The decentralized network was holding, the truth spreading like a virus through the Committee's pristine architecture.

"We gave them the spark," Elias whispered, wiping a smear of blood from his forehead. He forced himself to his feet, ignoring the throbbing ache in his skull. "Now we just have to survive the fire."
