# Elias at Sector Four - Phase 75
## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek land in the dark, waterlogged maintenance shaft as the Loyalist enforcers secure the substation above.
2. The team pushes through the treacherous, collapsing tunnels of Sector Four, aware that Vane's forces are using thermal scanners to track them.
3. They reach a massive, rusted junction that leads into the deeper, unmapped Undercity, only to hear the heavy, metallic clanking of a mechanized hunter drone echoing from the tunnel ahead.

## Chapter Text

The drop was punishing. Elias slammed into the concrete floor of the maintenance shaft, the impact driving the air from his lungs. Stagnant water splashed up around him, cold and reeking of ozone and decay.

Jarek landed a second later with a heavy splash, his rifle held high above the waterline. Kaelen followed, dropping lightly beside them, her sidearm already drawn and trained on the jagged opening above.

"Move," she hissed. "They have thermal. This deep, our heat signatures will light up like flares."

Elias scrambled to his feet, ignoring the sharp pain radiating from his knee. The tunnel stretched out before them, an endless throat of cracked concrete and rusted pipes. The only light came from the faint, pulsing blue glow of the counter-signal indicator on his wrist terminal. It was a fragile point of light in the suffocating dark.

Above them, the muffled shouts of the Loyalist enforcers echoed through the shaft. The heavy thud of boots shook dust from the ceiling, but none of Vane's hunters seemed eager to drop blindly into the abyss.

"They won't come down," Jarek said, his voice a low rumble. "They will seal the exit and drop charges."

"Then we make sure we aren't here when they do," Elias replied, leaning heavily against the curved wall as he pushed forward.

The trio moved through the treacherous terrain, wading through knee-deep sludge. The air grew thicker with every step, heavy with the oppressive weight of the Spire pressing down on them. Elias focused on the rhythm of his breathing, the splash of their boots, and the steady, comforting pulse of the counter-signal on his wrist.

"We need a cross-tunnel," Kaelen said, scanning the darkness ahead. "Something that splinters off this main arterial. If they drop hunter drones, this corridor is a kill zone."

As if summoned by her words, a low, metallic vibration hummed through the water. It wasn't a sound, but a physical sensation that rattled the fillings in Elias's teeth.

Jarek stopped abruptly, raising a hand.

Ahead of them, the tunnel widened into a massive, rusted junction. The space was cavernous, lost to the shadows. But from the far archway, a mechanical, rhythmic clanking echoed off the damp walls. A single, sweeping red optic beam sliced through the darkness, revealing the rusted pipes and stagnant water.

Vane hadn't waited for them to find an exit. She had already sent something down to find them.
