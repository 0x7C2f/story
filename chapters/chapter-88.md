# Elias at Quarantine Sector Alpha - Phase 88
## Outline

1. Elias triggers an overload in the isolated terminal to create a diversion.
2. Vane's forces hesitate as the core sparks and fills with smoke.
3. Kaelen and Jarek cover Elias as he escapes into the thermal exhaust port with the drive.

---

## Chapter Text

The red glow of the terminal cast long, violent shadows across the Archive core. Elias clutched the drive to his chest, the metal warm against his ribs. The enforcers were a wall of black armor and leveled rifles, closing the distance with methodical precision.

"Hold them!" Kaelen barked, her voice scraping against the acoustics of the vault. She fired a blinding volley of plasma into the vanguard, buying them a fraction of a second.

Elias didn't waste it. He slammed his free hand onto the interface, his fingers dancing across the haptic keys in a desperate sequence. He wasn't trying to access data anymore; he was rewriting the power distribution protocols of the isolated core.

*Warning: Overload imminent,* the system chimed, the polite voice jarring against the gunfire.

"Get back!" Elias yelled, diving toward the narrow access grate of the thermal exhaust port.

The terminal detonated. It wasn't a concussive blast, but a blinding eruption of sparks and acrid white smoke. The enforcers staggered, their visors struggling to compensate for the sudden thermal bloom. Jarek threw a fragmentation grenade into the confusion, the blast echoing like a thunderclap.

Elias hit the grate, scrambling into the dark, narrow tunnel. Kaelen followed, pulling Jarek in behind her. Behind them, the smoke cleared just enough for Elias to see Director Vane stepping through the shattered blast doors. Her face was an unreadable mask of cold fury as she surveyed the ruined terminal.

She had the Archive core. But Elias had the history. He gripped the drive tighter and kept crawling into the dark.
