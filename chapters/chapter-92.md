# Chapter 92

## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek flee into a rusted maintenance shaft to evade the heavy sentries deployed by Director Vane.
2. They hit a dead end and must choose between standing their ground or finding another desperate exit.
3. Kaelen discovers an old, sealed access hatch leading deeper into the forgotten levels.

---

## Chapter Text

The heavy, rhythmic thud of the sentries vibrated through the floor plates. Elias Thorne did not look back. He ran, his boots slipping on the slick metal of the Undercity corridor while Kaelen and Jarek kept pace right beside him. The air here was stale, thick with the scent of rust, ozone, and centuries of slow decay.

"Down here!" Kaelen shouted, her voice echoing in the gloom.

She vanished into a narrow opening in the wall—a rusted maintenance shaft. Elias followed. He scrambled through the jagged aperture before Jarek squeezed in last, his broad shoulders scraping loudly against the corroded metal. They tumbled into the darkness as the mechanical roar of the sentries passed by the opening, a terrifying surge of sound that slowly, mercifully faded into the distance.

Elias leaned against the damp wall, his chest heaving. "They missed the entrance. For now."

"We cannot stay here," Jarek rumbled. "This shaft is a dead end. We are boxed in."

Elias knew Jarek was right. Vane's forces would double back. He felt the cold weight of the physical drive in his pocket. The uncorrupted history was safe, but they were trapped. He pulled his wrist-comp up, but the screen showed only static. The signal interference was absolute this deep down.

"Look at this," Kaelen said softly from the darkness ahead.

Elias moved toward her voice. Kaelen was crouched before a massive, circular hatch set into the floor. It was heavily corroded, sealed by decades of neglect. A faint symbol, barely visible beneath the rust, marked its center—an emblem of the old world, long before the Committee took power.

"An old access port," Jarek noted, joining them. "Sealed from the other side, perhaps."

"It's our only way down," Elias said. He knelt beside the hatch, his fingers tracing the rusted edge. "If we can pry this open, we might find a path beneath Vane's perimeter. We drop off the grid completely."

"Or we drop into a flooded abyss," Kaelen countered, though she was already pulling a pry bar from her pack. "But it beats waiting for the sentries."

Elias grabbed the bar with her. "Then we dig."
