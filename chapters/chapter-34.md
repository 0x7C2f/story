# Chapter 34: The Dawn of the Archives

## Outline

1. Elias descends the Nexus Spire and observes the immediate aftermath of the city receiving the counter-broadcast.
2. The streets are chaotic as citizens confront conflicting memories; Committee loyalists try but fail to maintain control.
3. Elias steps into the crowd, ready to defend the truth.

---

## Chapter Text

The descent down the Nexus Spire was slow. Excruciatingly slow. The elevator hummed a steady, mechanical rhythm. It was a stark contrast to the storm breaking over the city outside. Through the reinforced glass of the exterior shaft, the sunrise bled across Sector One. Below him, New Providence was waking up, but not to the orchestrated dawn the Oversight Committee had planned.

The synchronization feed was dead. In its place, the raw, unedited archives surged through every terminal, datapad, and public display screen. People poured into the streets, gathering in disorganized clusters. Some shouted at municipal interfaces; others stood frozen, staring at screens scrolling with redacted history. The curated reality of the Committee was dissolving in real time.

Elias stepped out of the lift at the ground floor plaza. The usual pristine silence of the Spire's base was shattered. Sirens wailed. In the distance, a fractured chorus of panic and response echoed through the cavernous space. A squad of loyalist enforcers marched past the entrance, their armor scuffed and their formations loose. They shouted orders, but the citizens refused to move. The enforcers were no longer unquestioned authority figures; they were the architects of a stolen past, laid bare by the root key.

He pulled his collar up against the chill. Director Vane's control was broken. Vane would not concede with grace. The Committee would reorganize. They would deploy their remaining forces to contain the fallout, to brand the truth as a terrorist attack. The mesh network he and Jarek had built was holding, but it needed defense.

Elias merged into the crowd. The air tasted of ozone and impending conflict. He had opened the archive, but the war for New Providence's memory had only just begun.
