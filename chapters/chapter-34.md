## Chapter Text

Director Vane stood in the command center of Sector Vane, the ambient blue light of the holographic displays casting long shadows across her face. The displays, usually a grid of perfect order, were now a chaotic mess of error codes and fractured data streams. The synchronization broadcast had failed.

She did not panic. Paranoia had its uses, and her threat model had always accounted for the possibility of a breach, however remote. Archivist Thorne had proven to be that remote possibility.

She keyed a sequence into the primary console. "Initiate secondary fallback," she ordered the room, though only a handful of loyalists remained to hear it. "I want a complete sweep of the Outskirts perimeter. Deny him the Outskirts. Then deny him again. I want the log of every attempt. We will not wait for them to build a resistance; we will dismantle the foundation before it cures."

Her voice was an unhurried, administrative hum, carrying the absolute certainty of someone who authored the very systems now failing around her.

"Director," a loyalist technician said, his voice tight with an emotion Vane refused to acknowledge. "The central broadcast tower feed is severed. The grid is unspooling."

"I am aware of the schedule disruption," Vane said smoothly, adjusting the collar of her heavy matte coat. "History is not a record of what happened. It is a maintenance schedule. And someone has disrupted the schedule."

She stepped away from the console. The Spire was no longer a fortress; it was a target. The Committee's own records were unwinding, and soon the city would realize the extent of the rewrite.

"Lock down the Spire," Vane commanded. "Seal the sub-basement archive. Nothing goes in or out without my clearance."

She moved toward the door, her stride measured and deliberate. She would not be buried in the ruins of her own architecture. She would rebuild it from the outside in.
