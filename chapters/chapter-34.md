# Chapter 34: The Ascent

## Outline

1. Elias and Kaelen slip into the Nexus Spire's maintenance levels from the ventilation shaft.
2. They confront automated internal security drones operating without synchronization.
3. They disable the drones and breach the outer blast doors of the primary data core.

---

## Chapter Text

The ventilation shaft was dark and tight, carrying the harsh metallic scent of recycled air and ozone. Elias Thorne pulled himself up the vertical grating, his joints aching and his breath coming in shallow gasps. Below him, Kaelen moved with quiet efficiency, her pulse rifle slung tight against her back. The heavy transport bluff had failed at the final checkpoint, leaving them no choice but this subterranean crawl into the belly of the Nexus Spire.

Elias unlatched a heavy steel grate and shoved it aside. He rolled out onto the ferrocrete floor of the maintenance level, groaning as the impact jarred his bruised ribs. Kaelen followed a moment later, landing in a quiet crouch and bringing her weapon up to sweep the immediate area.

"We are in the tertiary sub-levels," Elias whispered, consulting his wrist monitor. The pale blue light painted his exhausted features. "The primary data core is three levels up. We need to reach the service elevator."

"Move fast, step light," Kaelen replied, her eyes scanning the dim corridor.

They advanced through the labyrinth of exposed pipes and heavy cabling. The air here vibrated with the raw power of the Spire's reactors. As they rounded a corner near a massive coolant nexus, the shadows shifted. Two automated security drones—sleek, thickly armored, and without a sound—dropped from their charging alcoves on the ceiling.

Kaelen reacted in a flash, diving behind a cluster of pressure valves as the drones opened fire. The corridor erupted in a storm of high-velocity plasma, scoring deep trenches in the ferrocrete. Elias scrambled for cover, dragging his hand across his neural rig to interface with the local node.

"They are uncoordinated!" Elias shouted over the deafening whine of the weapons. He accessed the diagnostic feed. "The counter-broadcast fractured their synchronization protocol. They are operating on isolated tactical loops."

"I don't care about their protocols!" Kaelen snapped, returning fire. Her pulse rounds deflected off the drones' heavy armor. "Shut them down!"

Elias bypassed the corrupted primary firewall and pushed a raw logic bomb into the local subnet. It was a crude, brutal tactic he would never have used as an archivist, but survival demanded adaptation. The drones convulsed as their isolated subroutines crashed. One plummeted to the floor, its optical sensors shorting out in a shower of sparks. The other spun wildly, firing a stray plasma bolt into a coolant line before it, too, powered down.

Freezing vapor hissed from the ruptured pipe, clouding the corridor. Kaelen emerged from cover, her rifle still trained on the smoking wreckage. She nodded to Elias. "Good work. Now let's find that elevator."

They pushed through the vapor, leaving the ruined drones behind. At the end of the corridor, the massive blast doors of the primary service elevator loomed. Elias placed his hand on the biometric scanner, hoping the chaos above would obscure his unauthorized access. The heavy doors ground open, revealing the dark shaft leading up to the heart of the Committee's secrets.
