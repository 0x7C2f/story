# Chapter 107

## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek search the sealed subterranean vault for an alternative exit route.
2. Vane, observing from the Spire, prepares to flood the vault with neuro-toxin to eliminate them.
3. Elias finds a compromised ventilation shaft, and they scramble to escape as the gas begins to vent.

---

## Chapter Text

The heavy blast doors offered no release whatsoever, remaining completely unyielding despite their efforts. Elias pressed his palm against the cold metal, feeling the faint, terrifying vibration of the towering Spire high above them. The red emergency lighting cast long, wavering shadows across the cracked concrete floor. "There has to be another way out," he muttered, turning back to the tangled mess of the severed trunks.

Jarek kicked the ruined chassis of a heavy sentry, his boots echoing sharply in the confined space. "They sealed us in tight. Unless your rig can magically weld steel, we are not going back up that lift shaft."

Kaelen moved along the perimeter wall. Her cybernetic arm traced the seams in the stonework with meticulous care, searching for any structural weakness that might allow them to force an exit. High above them, safe in the pristine observation decks, Director Vane watched the telemetry feed go dark. But she would not stop there. Vane always had a contingency.

"Look for maintenance hatches," Elias instructed, scanning the vaulted ceiling. "The cooling systems for the data cores must vent somewhere."

A soft hiss echoed through the chamber. A faint, sweet-smelling mist began to seep from the ventilation grates near the ceiling. Neuro-toxin. Vane was flooding the room, forcing their hand.

"There!" Kaelen pointed to a rusted grate partially obscured by fallen debris.

Elias and Jarek sprinted toward it. They heaved the rubble aside, adrenaline masking their exhaustion, fueled by the terrifying reality of the gas slowly filling the room. The mist thickened, stinging Elias's eyes. They pried the grate open, revealing a narrow, dark shaft. It was a desperate gamble, but the toxin left them no choice. They scrambled into the darkness, leaving the silent vault behind.
