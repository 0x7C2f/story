## Chapter Text

The cryptographic shielding around the central terminal dissolved into digital ash. Elias exhaled a harsh, jagged breath. The neural feedback receded, leaving a ringing echo in his skull. The console beneath his hands shifted from a hostile, locked state into an open administrative interface.

"We have the core," Elias said. His voice was raw, stripped of the usual procedural detachment. "I am accessing the master root keys."

Kaelen checked the hallway through the shattered remnants of the archive security doors. Her grip on the portable terminal was unyielding. "Work fast. Vane knows it was a diversion. The mesh network perimeter alarms are triggering in the upper corridors."

"Reserve enforcers," Jarek rumbled. The Scavenger ejected a spent shell from his heavy slug-thrower. It clattered against the obsidian floor. He took up a defensive stance near the primary chokepoint. "Let them come. I need the practice."

Elias ignored the impending threat and forced his focus onto the raw data streams cascading across his visual overlay. The uncompressed histories of millions of citizens—decades of systematic alteration, suppressed traumas, and fabricated loyalty—rested at his fingertips. It was the absolute truth of New Providence, heavy and volatile.

"Extracting the keys," Elias muttered. He routed the decryption sequence through his wrist unit. A progress bar materialized, inching forward with agonizing slowness.

High above, in the sterile silence of her command center, Director Vane watched the telemetry feed with cold, calculating detachment. The assault on Sector Five was contained, but the alert from the Nexus Archives burned like a flare on her tactical map.

"Seal the lower vaults," she ordered over the secure channel. Her voice was flat, carrying the absolute certainty of a system administrator terminating a rogue process. "Authorize lethal force for the reserve enforcers. Do not let them reach the broadcast grid."

"Too late," Elias whispered, though he knew she could not hear him. The progress bar hit completion. The master root keys compiled into a singular, undeniable string of code.

"Keys secured," Elias announced. He pivoted to the secondary console, the one wired directly into the city's underlying infrastructure. "I am bypassing the broadcast delay. Initiating the global system dump."

He slammed his hand onto the final execution command. The central terminal flared with blinding white light. A profound, resonant hum echoed through the chamber as petabytes of raw, unaltered memory surged upward, flooding the public grid. It bypassed the Spire's filters entirely, directly assaulting the neural links of every citizen in New Providence.

The heavy thud of combat boots echoed from the upper corridor. Kaelen raised her scanner. "Contacts. Multiple heavily armed signatures."

Jarek leveled his weapon. The first reserve enforcer rounded the corner, their synthetic armor gleaming under the harsh recessed lights.

Elias disconnected his neural link, gasping as the massive data transfer concluded. The truth was out. Now, they just had to survive the reaction.
