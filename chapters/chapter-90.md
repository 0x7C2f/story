# Elias at The Undercity - Phase 90
## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek try to find a safe path deeper into the Undercity maze while avoiding the sealed vents.
2. Vane's forces deploy tracking drones to scan the Undercity.
3. Elias and team realize they are being cornered and decide they need to fight back or establish a new hideout.

## Chapter Text

The Undercity was a labyrinth of rusted bulkheads, dripping condensation, and shadows that swallowed the dim emergency lighting. Elias Thorne moved with a desperate, halting rhythm, his hand instinctively hovering over the physical drive secured in his pocket. The blistering heat of the thermal exhaust port still clung to his clothes, a stark contrast to the damp, subterranean chill.

"The main arteries are going to be swarming," Kaelen muttered, her eyes scanning a cracked schematic displayed on her wrist-comp. The glow painted her sharp features in stark, erratic shadows. "If Vane sealed the vents, she knows exactly which sector we dropped into."

Jarek grunted, his heavy boots making entirely too much noise on the metal grating. "She won't send enforcers down here blind. Too many places to hide. Too many ways to set up an ambush."

"He's right," Elias said, his voice raspy from the heat and the exertion. "She'll send the drones first. Mapping the area, looking for thermal signatures."

As if summoned by his words, a low, oscillating hum vibrated through the metal framework of the tunnel. It wasn't the heavy, rhythmic thud of enforcer boots, but the high-pitched whine of repulsor lifts.

Elias froze. Kaelen instantly killed the light on her wrist-comp, plunging them into near-total darkness. They backed against the damp wall of a massive condensation pipe, holding their breath.

A sleek, angular drone drifted past the intersection ahead, its single red optical sensor sweeping back and forth like a pendulum. It was one of Vane's specialized seeker units—silent, fast, and equipped with a horrifying array of sensors.

"They're not just looking," Jarek whispered, his voice barely audible over the hum. "They're establishing a perimeter. Boxing us in."

Elias watched the red light fade down a side corridor, his mind racing. They had the uncorrupted history of New Providence, but it was useless if they died in this maze. Vane was tightening the noose, systematically cutting off every escape route. They couldn't just run; they had to break the perimeter before it closed completely.
