# Chapter 25

## Outline

1. Opening beat: Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek disembark from their transport and move stealthily through the heavily guarded Sector Six staging ground. They use the shadows of the massive marshaling yard to avoid enforcer patrols and automated sentry drones, relying on Elias's neural interface to spoof any stray sensor sweeps based on the captured crawler's data.

2. Escalation: They reach the central distribution structure housing the primary signal repeater node. The area is heavily fortified, and a full enforcer squad is guarding the access point. Jarek and Kaelen must coordinate a synchronized takedown of the guards while Elias hacks the reinforced blast door, fighting against the Committee's sophisticated localized encryption protocols that threaten to trigger a base-wide alarm.

3. Turn or decision: Once inside, they discover the repeater node is already spooling up its localized rewrite protocol sequence faster than anticipated. Elias realizes that simply blowing the power core might trigger a premature, uncontrolled localized memory wipe. They face a decision: Kaelen and Jarek must hold the chokepoint against reinforcing enforcers while Elias works under extreme pressure to first destabilize the synchronization matrix, allowing them to finally plant the satchel charges and destroy the node safely.
