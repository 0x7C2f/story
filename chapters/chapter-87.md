## Chapter Text

The tactical advantage evaporated in seconds. Vane's Loyalist enforcers pushed into the Archive core, their heavy armor absorbing Kaelen's suppressive fire. Jarek cursed, slamming a fresh magazine into his rifle as a plasma burst scorched the server rack beside him. The air smelled of ozone and melting synthetic polymers.

Elias did not look up. His fingers blurred across the haptic interface. Ninety-eight percent. Ninety-nine percent. The progress bar crept forward with agonizing slowness. Behind him, the staccato rhythm of the firefight shifted; the enforcers were no longer advancing cautiously—they were surging.

"Elias, we are out of time!" Kaelen shouted, her voice tight with strain.

"Done!" Elias slammed his palm onto the terminal, ripping the physical drive from the port. The console flared red as the connection severed. He clutched the warm metal of the drive to his chest just as the enforcers breached the inner perimeter, their weapons raised and steady. The download was complete, but they were trapped.
