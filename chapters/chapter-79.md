# Chapter 79

## Outline

1. Kaelen and Jarek engage the first wave of hunter subsystems, struggling to hold the perimeter while the uplink processes.
2. The terminal secures a hardline connection to the Nexus Archives, beginning the extraction of the quarantine schematics.
3. A heavier hunter unit breaches the platform, forcing the team to sever the connection early and plunge into an adjacent maintenance shaft.

---

## Chapter Text

The first hunter subsystem breached the platform with a metallic screech, its optical sensors flaring crimson in the station’s flickering amber light. It was a quadrupedal chassis, sleek and heavily armored, designed for tight-quarters suppression.

"Contact left!" Kaelen shouted, her rifle barking a sharp, staccato rhythm.

The armor-piercing rounds sparked against the drone's carapace, forcing it to stagger, but it recalibrated instantly. It lunged over a rusted turnstile, pneumatic limbs hissing.

Jarek intercepted it with a heavy swing of his scavenged repeater, the reinforced barrel colliding with the drone’s primary sensor cluster. The impact sheared the optics away, sending the machine crashing onto the cracked tiles in a shower of sparks and synthetic fluid.

"They are testing the perimeter," Jarek rumbled, chambering another slug. "More will follow."

At the terminal, Elias kept his hands steady on the clunky keyboard, ignoring the chaos erupting twenty meters behind him. The amber screen flickered, struggling to maintain the ancient handshake protocol. The progress bar crawled forward, a agonizingly slow accumulation of raw data packets.

"Come on," Elias muttered. "Just give me the schematics."

Another screech echoed from the tunnel entrance, higher and more sustained than the first. Two more quadrupedal hunters scrambled into the station, their chassis scraping against the plasteel archways.

"Elias!" Kaelen yelled over the gunfire. "We cannot hold this platform indefinitely."

"Thirty seconds!" he shot back.

The terminal chimed, a hollow, tinny sound that cut through the noise of the skirmish. A new directory materialized on the screen: *Quarantine Sector Alpha*. Elias’s pulse spiked. This was it. The architectural blueprints of Vane's deepest data vaults.

He initiated the transfer to his secondary drive, the archaic interface grinding against the modern encryption. The progress bar crept to forty percent. Fifty percent.

Then, the platform shuddered.

A massive, tracked sentry unit emerged from the darkness of the main tunnel, its heavy autocannon spooling up with a deafening whine. The reinforced plating on its chassis absorbed Jarek’s repeater slugs without a scratch.

"Heavy armor!" Jarek roared, diving behind a concrete pillar just as the sentry unit opened fire.

The concussive force of the autocannon shredded the turnstiles, sending shrapnel tearing through the air. A jagged piece of plasteel sparked against the terminal, shattering the right side of the monitor.

The transfer paused at sixty-two percent.

"Elias, we have to move!" Kaelen screamed, pulling a frag grenade from her webbing.

Elias ripped the secondary drive from the port, the terminal dying completely in a shower of blue sparks. "I have enough. Move! To the maintenance shaft!"
