## Chapter Text

Elias stood among the cooling slag and silent servos of the neutralized Goliath frames. The acrid scent of ozone and burnt insulation hung heavy in the subterranean air of Sector Five. Around him, the foundry workers maintained a cautious perimeter, gripping salvaged plasma cutters and heavy wrenches. They stared at the massive war machines that had moments ago threatened to drop their sector into the abyss.

"Telemetry is still flat," Kaelen said. She crouched beside the nearest Goliath's primary logic housing and tapped a sequence into her portable terminal. "The mesh network spoof held. They are dead weight."

Jarek moved past them. His heavy boots ground against the particulate dust. He inspected the locked ordnance bays with a critical eye. "Dead weight is heavy," the Scavenger muttered. "But it blocks the main approach. Vane will have to cut through her own armor to reach us here."

Elias turned away from the machines. His neural link pulsed with the fading echo of the encrypted command transmission. He had hijacked the localized failsafe and forced the Goliaths into a hard lockdown. The sheer volume of data passing through the makeshift resistance network had left him reeling.

Elias pressed a hand to his temple. "She knows it was a spoof, and she knows it originated from here."

"Then we hold the line." Jarek grunted, hefting a heavy slug-thrower.

"No," Elias countered. He opened the network visualization on his wrist unit. The holographic display projected a chaotic web of data nodes, mostly dark, but a few active streams trickled through from Sector One. "Look at the perimeter sweeps. When the Goliaths failed, she did not send secondary infantry to reinforce the breach."

Kaelen stepped closer. Her eyes narrowed as she read the data. "The thermal signatures are moving away from Sector Four. She is pulling the sweep teams back."

"Not back," Elias corrected. He traced the data flow. "She is concentrating them. Every available loyalist asset is converging on the Sector Five boundary. She intends to seal us in."

"A siege," Jarek observed.

Elias shook his head. "A containment protocol. But look at this void." He expanded the map, highlighting the central hub of New Providence. The Nexus Spire stood tall, surrounded by red alert indicators, but a secondary cluster of high-security nodes was entirely dark.

Kaelen inhaled sharply. "The Archives."

"She pulled the inner guard," Elias confirmed. The realization settled over him with precise, chilling clarity. "The most secure memory vault in the city. The repository of every unaltered record she has fought to suppress. She abandoned it to reinforce the perimeter here."

"Why?" Kaelen demanded. "The Archives are the core of her power. If we reach the primary servers..."

"She thinks we are building an army," Elias said. "She expects a protracted, kinetic war. She believes the memory liberation was the end of our technical offensive, and now we must fight for territory."

Jarek crossed his arms. "If she emptied the vault, we go to the vault."

Elias looked at the Scavenger leader, then back to Kaelen. The exhaustion in his bones receded, replaced by the procedural hum of a new plan. "The physical drives in the Archives contain the raw, uncompressed histories of the entire Oversight Committee. If we extract the master root keys from the central terminal, we bypass the Nexus Spire's broadcast delay entirely. We can force a total, irreversible system dump into the global grid."

"We need a transport," Kaelen said, her voice sharp with purpose. "And a distraction large enough to keep the perimeter forces looking outward."

Jarek grinned, a harsh, humorless expression. "The workers have plasma cutters. We can make noise."

Elias nodded. He closed the map display and synced his terminal with the mesh network. "Then we move. We strike the Archives while Vane surrounds an empty room."
