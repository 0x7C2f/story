# Chapter 2: The Escape

## Outline

1. Elias navigates the labyrinthine corridors of the Nexus Archives, avoiding standard patrol routes.
2. He narrowly evades a squad of Archive sentinels, realizing they are using lethal suppression protocols.
3. Elias escapes the facility into the rain-slicked streets of New Providence, realizing he is now a fugitive.

---

## Chapter Text

The siren’s wail was a digital scream, vibrating through the metal grate beneath Elias’s boots. He sprinted down the maintenance corridor, the stolen drive burning like a coal against his ribs inside his coat pocket. The Nexus Archives had been designed to mimic a labyrinth, an architectural deterrent against physical intrusion, but Elias knew the service routes better than the automated security routines.

Red emergency lighting strobed across the obsidian walls, casting long, distorted shadows. Ahead, a heavy blast door began to grind downward, sealing off Sector 4. Elias pushed his legs harder, his breath tearing in his chest. He slid the last few yards, boots skidding on the polished floor, and threw himself under the descending barricade. The metal edge slammed into the floor inches from his heels with a deafening clang, kicking up a puff of stale dust.

He scrambled to his feet, leaning against the cold wall to catch his breath. The corridor here was narrower, lined with cooling pipes for the main server clusters. Above him, the hum of the sentinels echoed—heavy, mechanical footsteps vibrating down the main concourses.

“Sector 4 sealed,” a synthetic voice boomed through the public address system, devoid of inflection. “Lethal suppression authorized for unauthorized personnel.”

Lethal suppression. Elias felt a cold sweat break out on his neck. The Oversight Committee didn't authorize lethal force for a simple data breach. Usually, they dispatched a retrieval team equipped with non-lethal neural disruptors. To authorize lethal suppression meant the corrupted fragment he carried was dangerous enough to warrant his immediate execution.

He didn't have much time. The sentinels would sweep the maintenance shafts next. He moved quickly, tracking the ambient temperature drop that indicated an exterior ventilation shaft. He found a grated access panel and wrestled it open, the rusted hinges shrieking in the quiet space.

Elias hauled himself into the narrow vent, crawling through the darkness. The air tasted of ozone and synthetic filter dust. After several minutes, he reached the exterior grate. He kicked it loose, and it clattered down into the alley below.

He tumbled out, landing hard on top of a pile of discarded transit crates, and rolled onto the rain-slicked ferrocrete of New Providence. The cold rain lashed his face, shocking him fully awake to his new reality. He was out of the Archives. He was alive. But as the distant sirens continued to wail, Elias knew he could never go back. He was a memory archivist no longer; he was a fugitive with a piece of history they were willing to kill for.
