# Chapter 66

## Outline

1. Elias leads Kaelen and Jarek into the inner transit arteries of Sector Four, trying to stay ahead of Vane's mapping algorithms.
2. The team bypasses a loyalist patrol by moving through a decommissioned magnetic rail shaft.

## Chapter Text

The inner transit arteries of Sector Four smelled of ozone and stale sweat, lit only by dim emergency lumen strips that cast sharp shadows across ribbed metal walls and heavy cables. Above them, the distant hum of the Spire’s primary cooling system vibrated as a steady pressure in the dead air.

Pressing his back against a cold stanchion, Elias Thorne clutched his powered-down interface deck tight to his chest. Because Director Vane was using telemetry from the automated turrets left behind in the hub to map their blind spots, a single network spike would let her loyalists triangulate their position in seconds.

"Movement, ahead," Kaelen murmured, crouching low beside a massive hydraulic dampener. Raising her custom submachine gun in a tight, practiced grip, the mercenary kept her focus completely fixed on the narrow chokepoint at the end of the maintenance corridor.

Jarek leaned forward to peer down the corridor, the subtle shift in his massive frame causing his heavy enforcer armor to scrape softly against the ferrocrete. "A sweep team," he rumbled in a low register. "Five marks. Heavy ordnance."

Elias calculated the intersection vectors mentally by pulling details from his encyclopedic recall of the city's physical infrastructure. Since the archives held the complete recorded history of New Providence's construction, he knew the patrol routes intimately. "We can't engage," he whispered to the others. "Vane deployed a predictive algorithm that feeds on our interference pattern, meaning if we fight here, her secondary patrols will box us in completely."

"Options, archivist," Kaelen demanded, her voice clipped in the tension.

"There's a decommissioned magnetic rail shaft parallel to this corridor," Elias replied, pointing down at a heavy, rusted access hatch set into the floor near Jarek's boots. "It was sealed forty years ago when the transit lines were re-routed, but it still bypasses the next two junctions."

Wasting no time on questions, Jarek gripped the heavy wheel of the access hatch, straining with his entire body weight. The rusted gears groaned in protest, and thick muscles corded tightly against his neck under the immense physical demand. Eventually the locked seal broke free, echoing like a gunshot in the confined space. Tearing the heavy iron plate aside with a final grunt, the massive enforcer revealed a deep shaft dropping into absolute blackness.

"Down," Kaelen ordered, already moving.

Grabbing the cold metal rungs, Elias climbed down after Kaelen to escape the sweep team. The dead, metallic air tasted heavily of rust on his tongue. Once his boots hit the curved, slick floor of the old rail tube, absolute darkness swallowed him.

A moment later, Jarek dropped hard beside him, pulling the hatch closed with a muffled clang. Kaelen sparked a low-light chem-stick, casting a sickly green glow over the curved walls of the tunnel.

The team moved quickly through the dead shaft. Keeping a steady pace, Elias felt his mind racing through the schematics. While the tunnel was supposed to lead beneath the inner checkpoint and emerge near the central router access node they needed to reach, a cold knot of dread began to tighten in his stomach. He realized Vane wasn't just reacting to their movements; she was shaping them.

"Stop," Elias breathed, coming to a sudden halt.

Kaelen froze instantly, raising a hand to stall Jarek. "What is it?"

Elias stared at the end of the tunnel, where the green light of the chem-stick reflected off a massive slab of solid ferrocrete that hadn't been in the archives. It blocked the shaft, reinforced with newly welded durasteel struts.

"It's a trap," Elias said, his voice tightening. He looked at the heavy seal, understanding the terrifying scope of Vane's countermove. "The blind spots we created... she didn't just map them. She used them to herd us here."

Above them, the heavy, metallic screech of a mag-lock disengaging echoed through the shaft.
