## Chapter Text

The subterranean vault echoed with the mechanical whine of heavy sentries descending the primary lift shaft. Elias Thorne wiped blood from his nose, his hand trembling as he disconnected his primary node from the blasted console. The physical data trunks were exposed, glowing with the ambient hum of the Spire's restricted network.

"They are sealing the upper blast doors," Kaelen said. Her cybernetic arm clicked as she recalibrated her sidearm, eyes fixed on the heavy sentry units dropping into the vault. "We have ninety seconds before we are entirely trapped."

Jarek stepped forward, lifting his heavy rifle. "Then we don't wait."

High above in the Nexus Spire, Director Vane watched the telemetry on her main screen. Her expression remained an administrative mask, though a muscle feathered at her jawline. The archivist was persistent. She keyed her comms. "All sentry units, execute purge protocol. Do not let them sever the primary trunks."
