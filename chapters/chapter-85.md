# Chapter 85

## Outline

1. Vane's assault team begins cutting through the sealed blast doors of the Archive core.

## Chapter Text

The heavy thud of breaching charges reverberated through the Archive core, shaking dust from the ceiling. Elias ignored the tremors. His hands flew across the rusted terminal keys, pushing the archaic system to extract the uncorrupted history faster. The download progress bar crept forward at an agonizing pace.

Kaelen checked the power pack on her rifle. She took up a defensive position behind a reinforced server rack. Jarek stood beside her, his jaw set in grim determination. They exchanged a silent look. They knew the blast doors would not hold forever.

High in the Nexus Spire, Director Vane watched the telemetry of the assault team. The first charge had failed to penetrate the primary gate. She ordered a second, heavier yield. The time for surgical precision had passed. She needed the archivist stopped before the download completed.
