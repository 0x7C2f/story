# Elias at The Nexus Spire - Phase 58
## Outline

1. The hard reset wave cascades through the upper corridors.
2. Moving through the hazardous maintenance shafts to avoid the sweeps.
3. Discovering a route out of the Spire back to the Undercity.

---

## Chapter Text

The digital static of Director Vane's hard reset crashed against Elias's shielded link. It struck as a tearing pressure behind his eyes, a violent frequency designed to scour the neural pathways of anyone caught in its radius.

"Stay low!" Kaelen commanded, her voice harsh in the narrow maintenance corridor. She grabbed Jarek's armored pauldron and hauled him toward a heavy structural crossbeam.

Elias braced himself against a cold ferrocrete wall as a secondary wave rippled through the Spire's primary conduits. The ambient lighting flickered and died, replaced by the sickly amber glow of emergency strips. Above them, the heavy, rhythmic thud of automated blast doors slamming shut echoed through the Citadel. Vane was sealing the upper levels.

"She is isolating the core," Elias said, his voice a rough whisper in the gloom. His hands shook as he checked the readout on his wrist terminal. "The purge is localized to the primary data arteries. If we stay off the main grid, we might avoid the sterilization."

Jarek shook his head, clearing his own neural interface. "They will not just leave the maintenance shafts unmonitored. We have sweep teams incoming. We need a way out."

The air in the shaft was stale, smelling faintly of chemical lemon and ozone. Kaelen scanned the gloom, her pulse pistol drawn. "There is an auxiliary exhaust vent three levels down. It connects to the old transit infrastructure before the Spire was expanded."

Elias nodded, swallowing hard. The structural vibrations of the sweep teams were growing louder, mechanized treads grinding against the deck plates above. "Lead the way."

They moved in silence through the labyrinthine sub-levels of the Nexus Spire. Every intersection was a risk, every shadow a potential loyalist squad. The silence was absolute save for the hum of the power grid and their own ragged breathing.

As they reached the access panel for the exhaust vent, a proximity alarm blared to life behind them.

"They found the breach," Kaelen said, her voice hard.

Jarek ripped the heavy panel from the wall with a grunt of effort, exposing a narrow, circular shaft dropping into darkness. "Down. Now."

Elias slid into the shaft, the metal cold against his hands, sliding into the deep dark, dropping toward the promise of the Undercity. The Spire was a fortress, and they had just kicked open its front door.
