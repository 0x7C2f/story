# Chapter 94

## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek move through the freezing, submerged tunnels of the Forgotten Depths.
2. They hear the distant echoes of Vane's enforcers beginning a manual sweep above them.
3. The team discovers a sealed bulkhead leading to an ancient, dormant transit tunnel.

---

## Chapter Text

The icy water gripped Elias's legs, stealing the warmth from his blood with alarming speed. He moved slowly, testing each step against the unseen floor of the submerged tunnel. The beam from his wrist-comp cast a pale circle in the immense darkness, revealing corroded pipes and rusted support struts holding back millions of tons of stone and upper-tier architecture.

"Keep to the center," Kaelen ordered, her voice flat in the damp air. "The edges look unstable. We don't know how deep the washout goes."

Jarek grunted, wading behind them like a displaced mountain. "These tunnels were built before the Committee. Maybe before the city itself. Nothing down here is mapped."

Elias paused, pulling the thick collar of his coat tighter. He pressed a hand to his temple, fighting the lingering ache from the heavy network dives. The uncorrupted history drive felt heavy against his ribs. They had slipped the digital net, but the physical reality of the Forgotten Depths offered a brutal exchange. Here, the Committee's pristine authority meant nothing against rot and cold.

A sudden, sharp reverberation echoed from the distant ceiling. Elias froze. The sound was faint, metallic, and rhythmically uneven.

"They aren't stopping," Kaelen observed, staring up into the impenetrable dark. "Vane is deploying manual sweeps. They're dropping enforcers into the access shafts."

"They won't find the hatch easily," Jarek argued, though his grip tightened on his heavy rifle. "But they will tear the grid apart looking."

"Then we need to move," Elias stated, pushing through the stagnant water. "If they find the access point, they'll flood this level with seeker drones. We need a path out of the primary drainage grid."

They pressed on, shivering in the oppressive cold. The tunnel eventually curved sharply, opening into a wider junction. Elias panned his light across the new space. Built into the far wall, covered in decades of calcified mineral buildup, was a massive, reinforced bulkhead door. Above it, barely visible under the grime, were the faded markings of a pre-Committee transit hub.

Elias stepped closer, brushing his fingers against the cold, dead metal. The seal was rusted shut, but the structure was intact. It was a dormant artery, long forgotten by the city above, and a potential lifeline in the dark.
