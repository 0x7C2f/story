## Chapter Text

The heavy blast doors groaned against the artificial feedback loop, their descent frozen mere meters above the deck. Elias scrambled beneath the massive slab of hardened synth-steel, the sharp scent of ozone and scorched metal flooding his senses. Behind them, the Nexus Archives echoed with the frantic, overlapping alarms of a system in catastrophic failure. The uncompressed histories—the raw truth of New Providence—were loose on the grid.

He checked his wrist display as Kaelen and Jarek cleared the threshold. The local sub-network was hemorrhaging data. Citizens across the sprawling undercity were waking to suppressed traumas and fabricated loyalties, their neural links overwhelmed by the sudden influx of unedited memory.

"The broadcast is holding," Elias said, his voice tight with the residual adrenaline of the breach. "The Spire's automated filters cannot process the volume. It is cascading through the lower sectors."

Kaelen knelt near a shattered support pillar, her weapon leveled back toward the compromised doorway. Her dark eyes scanned the flickering emergency lights. "Vane will not let this stand, Archivist. The moment she regains containment on her end, she will sterilize the sector."

Jarek grunted, reloading his heavy slug-thrower with practiced, brutal efficiency. The massive Scavenger ignored the superficial scorch marks on his armor. "Then we make sure she does not regain containment. We move."

They descended into the lower maintenance corridors of the Nexus Spire. The air grew dense and stale, thick with the metallic tang of coolant and the low, persistent thrum of the Spire's primary power conduits. Above them, Elias could feel the structural vibrations of heavy boots and mechanized treads—Vane's loyalist sweep teams, mobilizing to secure the compromised levels.

High in the sterile silence of the primary command center, Director Vane analyzed the staggering scope of the intrusion. Her tactical interface was a sea of red alerts. The memory extraction had bypassed every standard cryptographic seal. The resistance had weaponized the truth.

"The lower vaults are compromised," the synthetic voice of the central intelligence reported. "Data containment failure at seventy percent."

Vane did not blink. She reached out, her fingers resting lightly on the haptic override console. The situation was no longer about suppression; it was about survival. If the populace remembered, the Committee's authority would dissolve into anarchy.

"Initiate Contingency Protocol Alpha," Vane ordered. Her voice remained flat, devoid of panic.

"Protocol Alpha requires localized grid saturation," the intelligence warned. "Projected neural casualties in the lower sectors exceed acceptable parameters."

"Execute the order," Vane stated. "Isolate the primary transit arteries and flood the lower grid with a hard reset. Wipe the volatile memories from anyone connected."

Deep within the maintenance corridors, Elias felt the subtle shift in the ambient frequency. The low hum of the data streams spiked into a shrill, piercing whine. He staggered, clutching his head as a sharp wave of digital static washed over his own shielded link.

"What is that?" Jarek demanded, dropping to one knee as the frequency intensified.

"A hard reset," Elias managed to say, gritting his teeth against the sudden pressure. "Vane is purging the localized grid. She is trying to burn the memories out of their heads before they can share them."

Kaelen tightened her grip on her weapon, her jaw set in a hard line. The rules of engagement had just changed from containment to complete eradication.
