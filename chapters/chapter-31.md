# Chapter 31: The Infiltration Bluff

## Outline

1. Elias and Kaelen ride in the heavy transport, listening to the comms chatter from Jarek's diversion in the Outskirts.
2. They approach the outer perimeter checkpoint of the Nexus Spire.
3. The spoofed transponder gets them past the automated turrets, but an enforcer squad signals for a manual inspection, ending the scene on a sharp complication.

---

## Chapter Text

The interior of the heavy transport tasted of copper and stale ozone. Elias sat in the co-pilot's chair, his neural rig tethered to the vehicle's rudimentary diagnostic port. The mesh network was a chaotic roar of encrypted static, but through the noise, he could parse the distinct signatures of heavy ordnance impacting ferrocrete. The diversion in the Outskirts had begun.

"Jarek is engaging the sweep teams," Elias said, uncoupling the secondary feed to ease the pressure on his temples. "The loyalist chatter is frantic. Vane has committed her armor to the settlement."

Kaelen kept her hands loose on the control yoke. The heavy transport rattled over the scarred transit lanes leading out of Sector One, its massive treads eating the distance toward the central district. "If Vane committed the armor, she expects a prolonged engagement. It gives us a window, but we need to move fast."

Through the reinforced viewport, the Nexus Spire dominated the horizon. It was a monolithic spike of black steel and hardened glass, its lower levels swathed in the perpetual smog of the city's atmospheric cyclers. The spire was not just a building; it was the nerve center of the Committee, the fortress where the true history of New Providence was entombed.

Elias adjusted the spoofed transponder frequency. "The outer automated defenses will challenge us in two kilometers. The codes Kaelen pulled from Sector Six are active, but they were flagged during our escape. We have to hope the fractured synchronization network hasn't updated the local security node."

"Brace yourself," Kaelen warned, shifting the transport into a higher gear. "If the turrets register the flag, they won't ask for authorization. They will just turn us into slag."

The transport crossed the threshold. A sudden, jarring alert blared in the cabin as an invisible targeting laser swept across the hull. Elias stared at the diagnostic feed, his breath catching in his throat. The automated node queried the transponder. For a long, agonizing second, the system hung in a logic loop, unable to reconcile the valid codes with the severed command structure.

The alert silenced. The targeting laser disengaged.

"We are through," Elias said, a sharp exhale escaping him. "The outer ring accepted the spoof."

"Don't celebrate yet," Kaelen replied, her tone hard. She pointed through the viewport.

Ahead, the transit lane bottlenecked at a heavy plasteel barricade. The automated turrets were inactive, but a squad of Committee enforcers, clad in heavy riot gear, stepped out from a reinforced guardhouse. The lead enforcer raised a gauntleted hand, signaling the transport to halt, and activated a manual override switch on the barrier.

"The automated system let us pass, but the ground team is running a manual check," Kaelen said, her hand drifting toward the pulse pistol on her thigh.

Elias stared at the enforcer approaching the transport's reinforced door, a heavy plasma cutter slung over his shoulder.

"Get ready," Kaelen whispered.
