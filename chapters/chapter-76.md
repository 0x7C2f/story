# Elias at Sector Four - Phase 76
## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek flee from the mechanized hunter drone down a collapsing service artery.
2. They realize they cannot outrun it and must trigger a localized structural collapse to crush the machine.
3. Jarek detonates an old explosive charge, burying the drone under tons of concrete but sealing the passageway behind them, forcing them deeper into an unmapped sector of the Undercity.

## Chapter Text

The hunter drone surged forward, its metallic bulk tearing through the rusted framework of the junction. Water sprayed in high arcs as its treaded base ground into the submerged concrete. Elias didn't wait to see what kind of armament it carried. He pivoted, ignoring the screaming protest of his injured knee, and bolted down the narrowest service artery branching off the main corridor.

"Left!" Kaelen barked, her voice sharp over the deafening clatter of the machine.

Jarek covered their retreat, firing three short, controlled bursts from his rifle. The armor-piercing rounds sparked harmlessly against the drone's reinforced chassis. He swore, a low rumble of frustration, and sprinted after Elias and Kaelen.

The service artery was cramped, the ceiling sagging under the weight of centuries. Thick bundles of inactive fiber-optic cables hung like dead vines, brushing against Elias's shoulders as he ran. Behind them, the tunnel groaned. The drone was too large for the passageway, but it was forcing itself through anyway, shearing the walls and tearing down support beams with brute mechanical force.

"We cannot outrun it," Jarek stated, catching up to Elias. "It will tear the ceiling down on top of us."

"Then we bring the ceiling down on it," Kaelen replied, not breaking her stride. "Jarek, do you still have that mining charge from the settlement?"

Jarek grunted an affirmative. "It is old. Unstable."

"So is this tunnel," Kaelen said, sliding to a halt near a heavily corroded support pillar. "Set it here. Elias, get clear."

Elias limped past them, moving another thirty meters down the tunnel before turning back. The red optic beam of the hunter drone cut through the darkness, illuminating Kaelen and Jarek as they worked. The machine's auditory sensors locked onto them, and a harsh, synthesized warning klaxon blared from its external speakers.

"Done," Jarek announced, slapping the detonator against the concrete pillar.

He and Kaelen sprinted toward Elias just as the drone broke through the last major obstruction, its heavy weapons mounting spinning up with a high-pitched whine.

"Down!" Jarek yelled.

Elias threw himself flat into the stagnant water. The explosion was a concussive wave that knocked the breath from his lungs. The tunnel collapsed with a thunderous roar, a wall of jagged concrete and twisted rebar crashing down onto the hunter drone. The deafening noise was replaced by the chaotic grinding of metal being crushed under thousands of tons of stone.

Dust and debris filled the air, choking them. Elias pushed himself up, coughing violently. The passageway behind them was completely sealed. The red optic beam of the drone was gone, buried under the rubble.

"It is dead," Jarek said, shining his weapon light on the massive pile of debris.

"And so is our way back," Kaelen added, looking down the dark, unmapped tunnel that stretched ahead of them into the deep Undercity.
