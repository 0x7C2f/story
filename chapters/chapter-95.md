# Elias at The Forgotten Depths - Phase 95
## Outline

1. Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek pry open the dormant transit bulkhead in the Forgotten Depths.
2. They discover an ancient, intact mag-lev train sitting on a pristine track.
3. Elias finds a way to power up the train using his rig, providing a potential escape route.

---

## Chapter Text

The bulkhead gave way with a screech of tortured metal that seemed loud enough to reach the Spire. Elias stumbled backward as Jarek threw his entire weight against the lever they had rigged from a rusted pipe. A rush of stale, dry air poured out of the darkness, carrying the scent of ozone and preserved dust.

"Clear," Kaelen stated, her weapon immediately trained on the pitch-black opening.

Elias adjusted his wrist-comp, narrowing the beam to cut through the gloom. They stepped through the threshold, the sound of splashing water fading behind them. Before them lay a cavernous space, untouched by the rot and water of the outer depths.

Resting silently on a perfectly preserved track was a mag-lev train, sleek and angular. Its silver hull was coated in a thick layer of dust, but the underlying structure appeared intact. The Committee's logo was nowhere to be seen on its flanks.

"This predates the Directorate," Jarek muttered, his voice echoing softly in the massive chamber. "They sealed it all down here when they built the Spire."

Elias moved toward the front carriage, his boots leaving sharp prints in the dust. The control cabin door hung slightly ajar. He slipped inside, sweeping his light over the consoles. They were dead, screens cracked or coated in grime, but the primary interface port was a standard, pre-standardization model he recognized from early archive schematics.

"Can it move?" Kaelen asked, appearing in the doorway. She kept one eye on the dark tunnel ahead.

"The physical rail looks clear," Elias noted, brushing off the main console. "But there's no grid power." He tapped his neural rig, feeling the familiar hum against his temple. "I might be able to jump-start the localized battery banks if I tether my rig's power cell to the primary relay. It's a risk. If the feedback loop is closed incorrectly, it'll fry the rig and the drive."

"If Vane's enforcers find us in the water, we die," Jarek said, joining them. "Take the risk."

Elias nodded. He pulled the interface cable from his wrist and slotted it into the ancient console, holding his breath as he initiated the bypass sequence.
