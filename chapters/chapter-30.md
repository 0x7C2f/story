## Chapter Text

The settlement had transformed into a fortress in a matter of hours. The towering husks of discarded industrial lifters were dragged into position to block the main transit arteries leading into the Scrapyard. Welding torches sparked in the dark, throwing violent, erratic shadows across the ferrocrete, as scavengers reinforced the barricades.

Elias stood by a cracked monitor screen that Kaelen had salvaged from a downed patrol drone. He tapped the haptic interface, tracing a thin blue line across a structural schematic of the Nexus Spire. "The primary service shafts will be flooded with automated security, but Vane's forces will be pushing outwards. The subterranean maintenance levels should be lightly guarded."

Kaelen checked the power pack of her pulse rifle, slamming it home with a satisfying metallic click. "If Vane sends her heavy armor, she won't just try to breach the settlement. She will bombard it. The scavengers can hold them off, but it will be chaotic."

Jarek loomed behind them, his massive frame a solid anchor in the frantic activity. "Chaos is our domain," the Scavenger leader rumbled. He hoisted a heavy slug-thrower onto his shoulder. "My people know every blind spot and unstable floor in this sector. We will keep the Committee focused on the perimeter."

Elias looked at the diagnostic feed. The first signs of heavy atmospheric displacement were registering on the sensors. The Committee's drop ships were moving in. He met Kaelen's gaze, the headache from the neural feedback finally fading into a dull throb. "It's time."

"Make sure they see us," Jarek shouted to his lieutenants, his voice carrying over the din of construction. "Make them bleed for every inch of scrap."

As the first explosions echoed across the Outskirts, signaling the arrival of the loyalist sweep teams, Elias and Kaelen slipped into the shadows, moving against the tide of battle, charting their desperate path back to the heart of the Committee.
