# The Final Strike

## Outline

1. Elias and the team finalize the technical plan for the counter-broadcast.
2. The Committee forces breach the Undercity perimeter, forcing them to move quickly.
3. Elias initiates the network override, committing to the direct attack on Vane's system.

---

## Chapter Text

Elias dragged a heavy sleeve across his forehead. He wiped away the grimy film that seemed perpetually baked into the stagnant air of the Undercity. "The primary node is holding the load," he whispered to himself, his voice tight with anticipation. His fingers flew across the haptic interface. The portable rig glowed with a dull, aggressive blue in the shadows.

Kaelen leaned over his shoulder, her eyes fixed intently on the flickering, cascading data streams. "Are you sure it will push through the interference? Vane's mod lab is pumping out noise like there's no tomorrow."

"It has to," Elias replied. He rubbed the familiar ache beginning to bloom behind his temples. "We are employing a mesh approach, distributing the signal load evenly across the remaining active relays. If one node drops, the others pick up the slack without missing a single beat. The synchronization feed will shatter under a decentralized push, fracturing the control mechanisms that Vane spent years perfecting in the depths of the Spire."

Jarek checked the heavy slug-thrower resting against his thigh. He chambered a round with a loud clack. "You better hope it does. The Committee's sweeping forces just hit the upper perimeter of this sector. We don't have hours, and we might not even have minutes before they descend."

Elias took a slow breath. He centered himself amidst the rising chaos. This was not the quiet predictability of the Archives. There was no clean protocol here, no predictable routine to retreat into when things got difficult or frightening. He brought up the final executable. His thumb hovered over the commit prompt for a fraction of a heartbeat. "Then we don't wait. We launch now."
Jarek crouched low beside the rusted remnants of an ancient structural support pillar. He pressed the heavy slug-thrower tight against his shoulder. He checked his angles, his eyes sweeping the darkened corridor leading toward the outer perimeter. "Sweep team incoming," he hissed, his voice tight. "Three signatures. Manual augments. They are moving fast."

Kaelen unslung her rifle and moved to cover the opposite flank. She rested her weapon into the crook of a ruined metal bulkhead. "I have the left. Elias, you need to work faster. If they realize where the node is situated, they will call in heavy support and bury us under this rubble."

"I am moving as fast as the architecture allows," Elias muttered. His fingers flew across the haptic interface. The portable rig glowed with a dull, aggressive blue in the shadows. "The Spire's synchronization feed is heavily shielded. I am routing the counter-broadcast through five distinct localized mesh hubs to hide our exact location. It takes time."

A loud metallic crash echoed down the corridor, followed by the heavy, rhythmic thud of augmented boots hitting the ferrocrete floor. The Committee sweep team had breached the outer doors. The harsh glare of their tactical lights sliced through the gloom, casting long, frantic shadows across the crumbling walls.

"They are here," Jarek called out. He squeezed the trigger. The heavy slug-thrower roared to life, spitting fire and lead down the corridor. The lead enforcer staggered backward, their kinetic shielding flashing bright amber under the sudden assault.

Kaelen fired in short, controlled bursts, targeting the second enforcer as they attempted to flank Jarek's position. The confined space of the Undercity amplified the deafening staccato of their weapons. The air quickly grew thick with the acrid stench of cordite and ozone.

Elias tried to ignore the chaos erupting around him. He focused fully on the cascading data streams. The final execution protocol was nearly compiled. He watched the progress bar inch forward, each percentage point agonizingly slow. The mesh network was straining under the load, but it was holding.

"Elias!" Kaelen shouted over the din of the firefight. "We cannot hold them indefinitely! They are calling for reinforcements!"

Elias brought up the final executable. The progress bar hit one hundred percent. The prompt to initiate the override flashed green on his display. The fate of the city's memories rested fully on this single action.

"Hold your fire!" Elias yelled. He slammed his thumb down on the commit prompt.

The portable rig hummed with a sudden, intense surge of power. The dull blue glow flared blindingly bright for a fraction of a second before stabilizing. A shockwave of pure digital static rippled out from the primary node, washing over the Undercity and racing up the data lines toward the Nexus Spire.

The enforcers faltered, their augmented optics glitching violently as the localized tactical network collapsed around them. Jarek seized the opportunity, advancing fast to secure the corridor.

Elias slumped back against the cold stone wall, his breath coming in ragged gasps. The counter-broadcast was live. The synchronization feed was shattering. The real fight had finally begun.
