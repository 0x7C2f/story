# Chapter 13: The Outskirts

The journey beyond the city limits took them hours. They moved through the labyrinthine service tunnels that fed into the New Providence Outskirts, emerging just as the true dawn began to crest over the horizon. The air out here was different—less sterile, untainted by the ever-present hum of the city's synchronization grids.

Elias leaned heavily against the crumbling husk of a transport vehicle, his breath coming in ragged bursts. He stared back at the distant silhouette of the Nexus Spire. It stood like a dark spear against the morning sky, a monument to a shattered regime. The mass overwrite had failed, and in its place, the city was waking up to a reality it hadn't known it had lost.

"We can't stop," Kaelen said, her voice cutting through the stillness. She was already checking their scant supplies, her movements sharp and efficient. "The Committee's fractured. Not dead. Vane's loyalists sweep the perimeter by midday. They'll be looking for us."

"I know," Elias replied, his gaze still fixed on the city. "But for the first time... they're fighting a war they can't erase."

He touched his pocket, feeling the reassuring shape of the physical drive. It was empty now, its payload broadcast to the millions of nodes across New Providence. The truth was out there, unspooling in a million minds, sparking a fire that no black site protocol could extinguish.

"It's going to be a long road, Elias," Kaelen warned, though a rare, faint smile touched her lips.

"We have time," he said, turning away from the city at last and looking out into the expansive, unwritten terrain of the Outskirts. "We have the rest of our lives. And this time, we get to remember it all."
