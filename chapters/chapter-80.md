# Elias at The Undercity - Phase 80
## Outline

Escape into the maintenance shafts from the heavy sentry. Elias analyzes the partial schematics of Quarantine Sector Alpha and finds a hidden access point.

---

## Chapter Text

The maintenance shaft was a suffocatingly tight cylinder of rusted metal and damp air. Elias dropped first, his boots hitting the grate with a resounding clang that echoed up the tunnel. Kaelen followed a second later, her landing much quieter, and finally Jarek, who sealed the heavy hatch above them just as the autocannon roared to life again, the heavy rounds chewing into the plasteel flooring where they had stood moments before.

They descended in total darkness for what felt like hours. The only sound was their ragged breathing and the distant, rhythmic thrum of the Undercity's life support systems. When they finally reached a level platform, they collapsed against the curved walls.

Elias pulled the secondary drive from his pocket, the metal still warm. He booted up his portable deck, a cobbled-together device he'd scrounged from the relay station. He connected the drive, the small screen illuminating his face in a pale blue glow.

"Did we get it?" Kaelen asked, her voice tight with exhaustion.

Elias tapped furiously at the makeshift keyboard. "Part of it. The file structure is fragmented, but I've got the foundational schematics for Quarantine Sector Alpha."

He brought up a wireframe model of the facility. It was a massive, subterranean complex, isolated from the main grid and heavily fortified.

"Look at this," Elias pointed to a faint line branching off from the main cooling system. "It's an old, decommissioned thermal exhaust port. It leads straight into the lower sublevels. If it's still intact, we might have a physical access point."

Jarek leaned in, squinting at the screen. "That's a suicide run. The thermal shielding down there would fry a person in minutes."

"It's our only option," Elias said. "Vane is going to know we have this."

Miles above them, in the pristine command center of the Nexus Spire, Director Vane stood before a towering holographic display. The transit station feed was a mess of static and smoke, but the datalogs were clear.

"He pulled the architectural schematics," an aide reported, his voice trembling. "Specifically, the files pertaining to Quarantine Sector Alpha."

Vane's eyes narrowed. "He's not just running. He's planning an offensive."

She turned away from the display, her expression hardening. "Redeploy the Inner Guard. Pull them back from Sector Four and establish a perimeter around Sector Alpha. I want every thermal vent, every access hatch, every sub-level corridor locked down. If he wants to breach the Quarantine Zone, we will bury him in it."
