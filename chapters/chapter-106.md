# Chapter 106

## Outline

1. Elias frantically cuts the data trunks as Kaelen and Jarek hold the line against heavy sentries in the subterranean vault.
2. The heavy sentries breach the vault; Jarek destroys one, but they are overwhelmed.
3. Elias successfully severs the final trunk, halting the system wipe, but they are trapped in the crumbling vault.

## Chapter Text

Sparks showered the subterranean vault as Elias Thorne dragged his blade across the secondary data trunk. His temple throbbed, a rhythmic reminder of the strain on his neural rig. The heavy sentry in the lift shaft fired a torrent of suppressive fire, chewing through the concrete pillars.

"Get it done, Thorne," Jarek grunted, shouldering his heavy rifle. He fired three concise bursts, ripping the plating off the closest machine. The sentry staggered but kept advancing.

Kaelen knelt behind a shattered console, her cybernetic arm braced against the recoil of her sidearm. "They are rerouting power to the blast doors. They want us sealed inside."

Elias did not answer her. He had to finish this. He hooked his primary node into the final trunk, desperately searching through the tangled mess of wires for the physical kill switch that could end this. He ignored the burning in his lungs. Above them, in the pristine heights of the Nexus Spire, Director Vane would be watching this telemetry fail.

A secondary explosion rocked the chamber. Jarek shouted as a sentry's heavy ordnance clipped his cover. Elias grabbed the physical kill switch and twisted the final connector with everything he had, severing the link. The blue ambient light in the vault died instantly, plunging them into emergency crimson. The heavy sentries froze, their targeting optics dimming as the Spire's restricted network collapsed.

The archive was severed. The city was isolated. But the blast doors slammed shut, echoing with finality.
