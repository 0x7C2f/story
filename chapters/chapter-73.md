# Elias at Sector Four - Phase 73
## Outline

1. The Loyalist assault begins on the substation.
2. Jarek and Kaelen hold the line against heavy fire.
3. Elias executes the recursive loop, completing the counter-signal synchronization just as the door gives way.

## Chapter Text

The blast door buckled inward with a shrieking grind of metal. Dust rained from the ceiling, catching the amber light of the standby diodes. Outside, the harsh, staccato cracks of heavy munitions echoed through the fracture zone.

"They've brought a breacher charge!" Jarek shouted over the din, bracing his massive shoulder against the frame, his rifle leveled at the widening gap.

"Sixty seconds is up!" Kaelen fired a controlled burst through the breach, forcing the attackers to scramble for cover. "If you have a miracle, Elias, run it now!"

Elias didn't look up from the terminal. The crimson warning light pulsed like a dying heart, washing his face in desperate hues. His fingers danced across the haptic keys, splicing the final lines of the recursive script into the auxiliary power management subsystem. The hunter-killer algorithm was closing in, its digital jaws inches from severing the handshake.

"Almost there," Elias whispered, treating the console as an adversary. He hit the execute command.

For a terrifying second, the screen froze. The mechanical whine of the terminal pitched into a deafening scream. Then, the crimson light flickered, stuttered, and dissolved into a steady, unbroken blue.

"It took the bait!" Elias shouted, his voice cracking. "The hunter-killer is caught in the loop!"

On the monitor, the synchronization bar surged forward. Eighty percent. Ninety. One hundred.

A deep hum vibrated through the floorboards, carrying the frequency of a solid connection. The counter-signal had embedded into the central router. The mobile repeaters across Sector Six were synchronized. Director Vane’s mass rewrite protocol was dead in the water.

"It's done!" Elias turned toward the others, breathless. "The signal is broadcast—"

Before he could finish the sentence, a secondary charge detonated against the blast door. The explosion ripped the heavy metal hinges from the ferrocrete wall, throwing Jarek backward. Smoke and ozone flooded the room as the silhouettes of Loyalist enforcers poured through the opening.
