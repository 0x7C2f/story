# Elias at Quarantine Sector Alpha - Phase 86
## Outline

1. Vane's enforcers breach the Archive core and engage Kaelen and Jarek in a firefight.

## Chapter Text

The primary gate buckled inwards with a shrieking tear of metal as the heavier charge detonated. Smoke plumed into the frigid air of the Archive core, obscuring the hulking silhouettes of Vane's Loyalist enforcers pouring through the breach. Kaelen opened fire immediately, her rifle's staccato bursts lighting up the haze. Jarek provided suppressing fire from the flank, aiming for the joints in their heavy armor. Behind the server racks, Elias gritted his teeth, his fingers dancing across the terminal. The uncorrupted history transfer reached ninety percent. High above, Director Vane watched the telemetry feed, a cold satisfaction settling in as her forces pushed deeper into the core.
