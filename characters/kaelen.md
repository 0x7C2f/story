# Kaelen

## Concept

A resourceful information broker and Elias's primary contact in the Undercity. Kaelen operates outside the corporate grid, dealing in raw data, forged credentials, and forgotten histories.

## Backstory

Formerly a junior analyst for a minor data conglomerate, Kaelen was ousted after uncovering widespread algorithmic manipulation in public records. Disillusioned with the system, they retreated to the Undercity, building a hidden network of informants and rogue tech to survive. Kaelen is deeply paranoid but possesses a strong, albeit cynical, sense of justice.

## Appearance

Scruffy and practical. Kaelen wears layers of dull, protective clothing designed to blend into the shadows of Sector Four. They carry an array of modified tech implants, visible as slight metallic ridges along their jawline and neck.

## Voice and Mannerisms

Kaelen speaks quickly, often in clipped, cynical sentences, prioritizing efficiency over politeness. They have a habit of constantly checking their surroundings and tapping their fingers against their arm when deep in thought.

## Desires and Fears

**Desire:** To tear down the corporate monopoly on truth and expose the systemic manipulation of memory.
**Fear:** Being discovered by the enforcers and having their own memories erased or rewritten.
