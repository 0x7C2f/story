# Elias Thorne

## Appearance

Wears a trench coat. Has a neural jack at the base of his skull. Always looks like he hasn't had enough sleep. Carries a plasma pistol.

## Personality & Traits

Pragmatic, cynical, and highly paranoid. Keeps his cards close to his chest and trusts no one. A survivor in the lower levels.

## Backstory

A skilled memory thief who operates in the lower levels of Neo-Veridia. Something in his past keeps him grounded in the slums of Sector Four, trying to survive day to day.

## Motivations & Goals

External want: A big enough payout to escape the lower levels.
Internal need: To uncover the truth about his past and his true identity.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Pragmatic, cynical, sharp.
"Depends on who's asking."
"I don't do scrubs in the alley, pal."
