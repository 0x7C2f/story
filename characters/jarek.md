# Jarek

## Appearance

Jarek is a rugged, weather-beaten individual, bearing the scars of survival in the Outskirts. He has calculating eyes, and wears mismatched armor pieced together from discarded city enforcer gear and scavenged metal. His hands are calloused, stained with rust and grease.

## Personality & Traits

Pragmatic, deeply suspicious of outsiders (especially city dwellers), but fiercely protective of his community. He values actions and tangible benefits over promises and ideology. He speaks plainly and bluntly.

## Backstory

Once a low-level maintenance worker in New Providence, Jarek was exiled to the Outskirts years ago after discovering too much about the city's resource allocation inequalities. He survived by banding together with other exiles, eventually becoming a respected leader in the Scrapyard Settlement.

## Motivations & Goals

His primary goal is the survival and prosperity of the Scrapyard Settlement. He wants to secure resources to outlast the harsh conditions and potential retaliation from the city.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

"Words don't keep the cold out. What can you actually do for us?"
"The city throws away its problems. We make them useful."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Wary and suspicious of Elias and Kaelen.
- **Key turning points:** Seeing the tangible proof of their actions against the city.
- **Ending state:** Willing to form a tenuous alliance based on mutual survival.
