# Batch Protocol

## Definition

The "Batch Protocol" (also known informally as "memory splicing") is a highly classified operation executed by the Oversight Committee. It involves the systematic rewriting of digital memories on an industrial scale. Rather than targeting individuals one by one, the protocol utilizes an automated algorithm to overwrite the neural records of thousands of citizens simultaneously, changing the baseline reality and collective history of New Providence.

## Usage Notes

Use "Batch Protocol" when referring to the official, industrial-scale memory modification process used by the Oversight Committee. The term "memory splicing" can be used as a colloquial or technical shorthand by characters like Elias or Kaelen when discussing the granular mechanics of altering an individual's specific memory. The protocol leaves subtle traces (such as static and artificial coding structures underneath the visual layer), which is how Elias first detects it.
