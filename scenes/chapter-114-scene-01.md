# Vane's Counterattack

## Purpose

Director Vane acknowledges her defeat as the Committee's control over the city crumbles, and she resolves to retreat and survive in the new reality created by the resistance's uncorrupted history broadcast. This serves as the resolution to the "vane-countermove" plot arc.

## Continuity Notes
