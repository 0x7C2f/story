# Vane Assesses the Situation

## Purpose

To introduce Director Vane on the page, establishing her appearance, personality, and immediate response to the failure of Project: Synchronization, setting up her counter-offensive against Elias and Kaelen.

## Continuity Notes

- **Character State:** Director Vane is established in her command center (Sector Vane in the Nexus Spire). She is calm, paranoid, and focused.
- **Knowledge State:** Vane knows Elias and Kaelen have fled to the Outskirts and deploys loyalist teams to sweep the perimeter. She recognizes the fracture in the Committee's command structure but remains determined to reassert control.
- **Timeline Facts:** Takes place on the morning of Day 7, coordinating the lockdown of the Outskirts perimeter by midday.
