# Chapter 79 Scene 1

## Purpose

Shows the immediate escalation of Vane's hunter-killer subsystems pushing into the deep Undercity, and establishes the crucial plot progression of Elias extracting partial schematics of Quarantine Sector Alpha before being forced to flee from a heavy sentry unit.


## Continuity Notes

- **elias-thorne**: Exhausted and operating a pre-Committee terminal. Successfully initiates a hardline transfer and obtains a partial schematic of Quarantine Sector Alpha.
- **kaelen**: Engages standard quadrupedal hunter subsystems with her rifle and covers the retreat using a frag grenade.
- **jarek**: Destroys a hunter subsystem in melee with his repeater. Realizes their weapons are ineffective against the heavy sentry.
- **director-vane**: Deploys a heavy tracked sentry unit with an autocannon to the deep Undercity.
