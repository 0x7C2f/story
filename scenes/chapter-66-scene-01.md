# Chapter 66 Scene 1

## Purpose

The vanguard pushes through the outer defenses of Sector Four. Jarek and Kaelen hold off the Committee Enforcers while Elias forces open a security gate to advance. Vane counters Elias's slicing efforts and attempts to fry his neural link, cementing the realization that she is using the combat telemetry to hunt him directly.

## Continuity Notes

- Elias-Thorne is physically strained, experiencing a sharp temple ache and shaking hands after Vane's counter-attack.
- Kaelen and Jarek provide suppressive fire and advance through the transit hub.
- The resistance successfully breaches the security gate into the transit arteries, but Elias realizes they are walking into a trap set by Vane.
