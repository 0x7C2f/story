# Sector Six Staging Ground Infiltration

## Purpose

The resistance successfully infiltrates the Sector Six staging ground and destroys the primary signal repeater, crippling Director Vane's mobile rewrite protocol before it can be fully deployed.

## Continuity Notes

- **Elias Thorne**: Successfully splices the crawler's clearance to bypass the outer checkpoint. Hacks the staging ground grid to kill the floodlights and provides tactical support. Downloads the deployment logs from the primary signal repeater before it is destroyed.
- **Kaelen**: Provides armed overwatch and takes down loyalist guards during the infiltration.
- **Jarek**: Pilots the captured crawler and plants repurposed munitions to destroy the primary signal repeater.
- **Object State**: The primary signal repeater at the Sector Six staging ground is destroyed. The captured crawler is still active and used for extraction. The deployment logs are extracted to a physical drive.
