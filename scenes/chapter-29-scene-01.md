# The Settlement Defense

## Purpose

Elias initiates a decoy signal to draw the full force of Director Vane's loyalist sweep teams down on the Scrapyard Settlement. Jarek and the scavengers hold the line in a desperate diversionary defense, allowing Elias and Kaelen to slip away through the southern drainage network to return to the Nexus Spire.

## Continuity Notes

- The Scrapyard Settlement is now an active warzone, subjected to heavy artillery bombardment.
- Elias and Kaelen are entering a subterranean route (southern drainage network) where comms will be cut off.
- The loyalist forces believe the central spire is broadcasting the root key signature, focusing their assault there.
