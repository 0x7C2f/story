# Chapter 98 Scene 1

## Purpose

Elias and his team execute a risky distraction in the heavily fortified Sector Six staging ground to reach the central distribution hub, advancing both The Memory Liberation and Director Vane Countermove arcs.

## Outline

1. Opening beat: From their hidden vantage point in the access shaft, Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek finalize their plan to breach the central distribution hub amidst the heavy enforcer presence.
2. Escalation: Jarek triggers an explosion in one of the transport refueling lines, creating chaos. Kaelen provides cover fire while Elias sprints across the illuminated yard toward the hub.
3. Turn or decision: Elias reaches the reinforced door of the hub just as a heavy sentry spots him. He realizes he cannot pick the lock in time and must override it forcefully with his neural rig, risking detection by Vane.
