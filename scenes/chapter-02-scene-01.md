# Chapter 2, Scene 1: The Descent

## Outline

Elias evades the Nexus Archives' security perimeters and descends into the labyrinthine depths of the Undercity. He seeks refuge in an abandoned maintenance sector, out of reach from the corporate monitoring grid. Hooking up the physical drive to a jury-rigged terminal, he begins to untangle the encrypted raw data of memory M-774-B. The decryption reveals a black-site modification lab overseen by a high-ranking director from the Oversight Committee.
