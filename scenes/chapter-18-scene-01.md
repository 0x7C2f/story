# The Salvage Run

## Purpose

To execute the salvage run into the Dead Zone, establishing the harsh conditions of the area and the nature of the pre-collapse infrastructure. It secures the necessary hardware (Signal Repeaters) required to build the decentralized mesh network back to the city.

## Continuity Notes

- **Character State:** Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek venture into the irradiated Dead Zone and successfully break into the abandoned relay station. They remain uninjured, protected by their gear, but aware of the climbing radiation.
- **Object State:** Six military-grade signal repeaters are salvaged from the station's reinforced cabinet. Their status becomes 'active'.
- **Knowledge State:** Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek confirm that the hardware is viable and heavily shielded. They know they now possess the means to punch a signal through the city's perimeter jamming.
- **Timeline Facts:** This establishes the successful salvage run on the day following their agreement to organize the resistance.
