# Escape and Implications

## Purpose

To transition Elias and Kaelen from the high-stakes infiltration back to the safety of the Undercity, and to recontextualize their mission. The root key is no longer just evidence; it is the only way to hijack Director Vane's impending mass overwrite protocol and broadcast the truth to the entire city.

## Continuity Notes

- **Character state:** Elias and Kaelen escape the Nexus Spire and relocate to a safehouse in the Undercity. Elias is physically recovering from the neural dive (nosebleed subsiding, trembling).
- **Object state:** The root key (on the physical drive/rig) is still in Elias's possession and successfully retrieved from Vane's archive.
- **Knowledge changes:** Elias and Kaelen now fully understand Project: Synchronization—a city-wide mass memory overwrite protocol. They realize the root key has the clearance needed to hijack the broadcast network and push the unaltered files instead.
