# Entering Sector Six Maintenance Hub

## Purpose

The ancient train carries Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek directly into the abandoned maintenance sublevels of the Sector Six staging ground. They must disembark before the train crashes into a sealed barricade and find a way up into the staging ground itself to plan their counter-offensive, while realizing Vane's forces have already fortified the area above.

## Continuity Notes

- Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek arrive beneath the Sector Six Staging Ground via the ancient train.
- They discover the staging ground above is heavily fortified by Vane's loyalists.
- Elias realizes the downloaded history needs to be broadcast from the staging ground's central repeater.
