# The Horizon Unbroken

## Purpose

To establish the immediate aftermath of the Nexus Spire run and the counter-broadcast, showing the scavengers receiving the truth while revealing Vane's tactical retreat to consolidate her forces.

## Continuity Notes

- Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek are back at the Scrapyard Settlement.
- The mesh network is active and broadcasting true memories to the scavengers.
- Kaelen discovers through comms interception that Vane's loyalist forces are retreating from the Outskirts perimeter to form a defensive firewall around the core sectors.
- Elias realizes Vane is consolidating to build a new protocol and commits to continuing the offensive.
