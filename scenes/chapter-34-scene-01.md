# Scene 1: The Fallout

## Purpose

To show the immediate consequence of the counter-broadcast on the city and to reunite Elias and Kaelen for their next steps.

## Action

Elias descends from the Nexus Spire and walks into Sector One. He observes the chaotic, unfiltered memories bleeding onto the public grid and the stunned reactions of the citizens. Kaelen finds him, reporting that Jarek is holding the perimeter but they need to move before Vane's loyalists retaliate.

## Aftermath

Elias and Kaelen agree to leave the immediate area and prepare for the long war ahead. The memory liberation is successful, but Vane's countermove is imminent.
