# The Infiltration

## Purpose

To execute the heist on Director Vane's personal archive, retrieve the root key necessary to decode the batch manifest, and uncover the terrifying scope of the Oversight Committee's endgame (the city-wide memory synchronization protocol).

## Continuity Notes

- Elias and Kaelen successfully infiltrate the Nexus Spire using a localized EMP.
- Elias interfaces with a hostile, air-gapped node, risking severe neural damage.
- They acquire the root key.
- Elias discovers "Project: Synchronization," a plan to overwrite the entire city's collective memory simultaneously.
