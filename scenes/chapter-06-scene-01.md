# Manifest Analysis

## Purpose

Elias and Kaelen regroup in a safehouse in the Undercity to analyze the partial batch manifest they extracted. They discover the specific methodology the Oversight Committee uses, but realize the file is missing the critical encryption key needed to broadcast the proof to the public. They must decide their next steps.

## Continuity Notes

- Elias and Kaelen successfully escaped the Sector Four black site and are back in the Undercity.
- Elias now possesses the `partial-batch-manifest` artifact.
- They know the Committee's methodology but lack the final key.
