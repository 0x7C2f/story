# The Ambush

## Purpose

To resolve the immediate threat of the loyalist sweep teams by executing a coordinated ambush at the old transit bridge, resulting in the capture of a Committee transport and securing the resistance's position.

## Continuity Notes

- Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek use the tactical data gathered in Chapter 21 to set up an ambush at the eastern ravine's old transit bridge.
- The resistance successfully neutralizes the four-man loyalist squad.
- They capture a functional loyalist transport vehicle.
- Director Vane's sweep teams are temporarily halted in the Outskirts perimeter.
