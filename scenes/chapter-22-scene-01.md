# The Transit Bridge Ambush

Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek position themselves in the rusted undercarriage of the old transit bridge. Using the mesh network, Elias tracks the loyalist sweep team's approach. As the heavy transport crawls across the debris-choked span, Jarek initiates the ambush with an EMP charge salvaged from the Dead Zone.

The blast disables the transport and the escort drones. Kaelen and Jarek neutralize the disoriented enforcers while Elias interfaces with the transport's dormant systems, bypassing the security lockout. They secure the vehicle and its cache of supplies and communications gear. The resistance now has a mobile asset and a deeper tap into the Committee's network.
