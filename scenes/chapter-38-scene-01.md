# The Strike on Sector Six

## Purpose

Director Vane's forces launch a concentrated assault on the Sector Six staging ground, overwhelming the resistance. Elias is forced to destroy their salvaged signal repeaters to prevent Vane from tracking them back to the Outskirts.

## Continuity Notes

The resistance loses their primary staging ground and mesh network infrastructure but manages to escape the immediate assault.
