# Chapter 41 Scene 1

Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek maneuver the damaged heavy transport into an isolated, rusted canyon in the Outskirts. Jarek physically strips the transport's heavy armor to expose the transmitter array, while Kaelen rewires the communication suite for decentralized broadcast. Elias synchronizes the raw transmission with the scattered dormant nodes, initiating the new, untraceable mesh network just as Committee interceptors begin sweeping the adjacent sectors.
