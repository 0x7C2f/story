# Chapter 45 Scene 1

## Purpose

Elias, Kaelen, and Jarek emerge from the smuggler tunnels into the industrial haze of Sector Five. They find the district in chaos as the workers, their memories restored by the mesh broadcast, have begun to riot against the Committee. A loyalist sweep team arrives, but the trio uses the ensuing conflict between the workers and the loyalists to slip away deeper into the city.
