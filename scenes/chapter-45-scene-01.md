# Chapter 45 Scene 1

## Purpose

Elias makes a critical choice to stop running and start leading. Instead of slipping past the unrest in Sector Five, he risks exposure to turn the confused factory workers into an active resistance network, shifting the group's objective from sheer survival to organized retaliation.
