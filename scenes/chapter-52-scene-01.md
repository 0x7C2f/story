# Vane's Next Move

## Purpose

Director Vane learns that Elias Thorne successfully stopped the Goliath assault frames in Sector Five using the mesh network. Instead of retreating or rethinking her strategy, she decides to escalate, pulling all available assets from other locations, including the Archives' inner guard, to focus on destroying the resistance in Sector Five.

## Continuity Notes

- **Character State:** Director Vane remains cold, administrative, and unpanicked, but her strategy shifts from sweeping, elegant protocols to direct, concentrated brute force.
- **Knowledge Changes:** Director Vane knows that Elias Thorne spoofed the transmission to lock the Goliaths. She knows the transmission origin is deep within Sector Five.
- **Timeline Facts:** Vane orders the rerouting of all remaining loyalist sweep teams and the inner guard from the Archives to the Sector Five boundary.
