# Voice Sample

This file serves as a quick-read companion to [style-sheet.md](style-sheet.md). It establishes the house style and voice based on the strongest existing prose.

## Voice notes

The voice is gritty, atmospheric, and cynical, characteristic of cyberpunk. Sentences balance sharp, sensory descriptions with fast-paced action. Metaphors are mechanical or urban (e.g., "rain... congealed," "plunging barehanded into a live fuse box"). The tone reflects a weary protagonist navigating a hostile, high-tech world.

## Demonstration

The rain in Neo-Veridia didn't fall so much as it congealed. It clung to the neon signs of the lower levels, turning their garish pinks and harsh blues into smeared, greasy streaks against the perpetual twilight. Elias Thorne stood beneath the flickering overhang of a defunct noodle stand, the collar of his trench coat turned up against the damp, his neural jack itching at the base of his skull. It always did when he was expecting a job.

He checked his chrono. Midnight. The agreed-upon time. The alley smelled of ozone, old synth-spices, and the unmistakable metallic tang of desperation. Down here in Sector Four, desperation was the only currency that held its value.

Footsteps splashed in the distance, uneven and hurried. Elias shifted his weight, his right hand slipping instinctively into his pocket, resting on the cold grip of his plasma pistol. He was a mnemonic extractor, a memory thief by another name, and his line of work didn't exactly cultivate trust.

A figure emerged from the smog, cloaked in a heavy, drab parka that hid everything but the anxious set of their shoulders. As the figure stepped into the dim light of the overhang, Elias recognized the jittery movements of a regular client, though he couldn't place the face under the hood.

"You Thorne?" the figure asked, their voice synthesized, flat, and metallic. A voice modifier. Paranoid. Elias liked paranoid. It usually meant they had something worth paying well to keep quiet.
